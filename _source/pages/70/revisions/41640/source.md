From the [SolSeedCreed](/SolSeedCreed)

*
[Empathy](/Empathy) is transcendent.
Without [Empathy](/Empathy), 
[SelfLove](/SelfLove) and [Wisdom](/Wisdom) are evil!
*

[Empathy](/Empathy) is the identification with and understanding of another's situation, feelings and motives.

Like [SelfLove](/SelfLove) and [Wisdom](/Wisdom), [Empathy](/Empathy) is a fundamental attribute of human nature.  While [SelfLove](/SelfLove) ultimately motivates all action, [Empathy](/Empathy) allows us to direct [SelfLove](/SelfLove) toward action that benefits the collective.  [Empathy](/Empathy) allows us to understand and value others --- it is via [Empathy](/Empathy) that we transcend self.    Culture and society spring from [Empathy](/Empathy). Without [Empathy](/Empathy) the deep understanding and trust so necessary for collective action cannot exist.  It is [Empathy](/Empathy) that spurs us to value the needs of others and consider how our actions might impact them.

Ward pointed out an interesting article in Science that indicates empathy goes at least as deep as new world monkeys.  Check out [The Perception of Rational, Goal-Directed Action in Nonhuman Primates](http://www.sciencemag.org/cgi/content/abstract/317/5843/1402).  I especially appreciated this quote interpreting their results "Thus, action perception must also consist of a mechanism that evaluates action means in relation to goals, and places this analysis into a broader context that entails constraints imposed by the current environmental situation".  Basically the monkeys were answering the question, "If I were them, why would I have done what they just did?" 08:42, 25 September 2007 (PDT)
