#### Brandon
During the next year I want to spend several hours every week working toward friendly AI. Working toward friendly AI follows an arc of development that begins with a literature survey phase.  Doing the literature survey consists of looping over papers:

- Identify a core paper that I want to dissect
- As I read the paper, tear it apart and put the pieces into a public facing wiki
- As I collect the pieces from these various papers I will build a world of AI concepts with various themed tours through that world.
- Select the next paper.

I’m not sure what phase two looks like, but I think it involves some coding and continued surveying.
