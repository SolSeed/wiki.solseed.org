**SolSeed: Pick a Future** is a six-minute music video created by [Ben](/Ben), then abandoned because we couldn't get proper permission to use the music track, "Down to Earth" by Peter Gabriel, which played over the credits of the movie WALL-E.  We also wanted to play the last verse of "After the Gold Rush" by Neil Young during the credits.
