#### Kindred Map
Brandon wants to make a conceptual map of kindred groups and how they intersect
- People could participate by making rank-ordered lists of values, and finding people and groups with similar lists
- You can also say what you think another group or person's values are (to explain why you feel aligned with them)
- Start with a spreadsheet, then build up to a graphical thing like a Venn diagram
- Brandon has more energy for this than for [Kindred 2015](/Kindred_2015) because he wants to get to know all these new contacts before inviting them to fly across the country

Eric observes that we might find a need to focus on one nexus of interest at a time, e.g. naturalistic religion (which has very little overlap with space)
