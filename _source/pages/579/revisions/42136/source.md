#### Understanding Wiki Technology
- <s>edit.</s>
- create a link to a page on the wiki.
- create a link to a page on another site.
- follow along with the Recent Changes to pages on the wiki.
- examine the contributions of another participant.
- find and interpret the difference between a wiki page before and after someone's edit.
- upload an image to the wiki and include that image in a wiki page.
- find help when you want to do something you don't already know how to do.
