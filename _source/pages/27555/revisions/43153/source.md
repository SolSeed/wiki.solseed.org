Our 2016 Spring Equinox celebration was held on Sunday March 20th in Redmond and Gatineau. We added board games as a new element, with the West Coast contingent playing [Evolution](http://www.northstargames.com/products/evolution) and the Saumurs playing [Terra Mystica: Fire & Ice](https://diceygoblin.com/blog/terra-mystica-fire-ice-review/).
