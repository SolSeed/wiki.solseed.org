[Lauren Oya Olamina](/Lauren_Oya_Olamina) is the founder of the fictional religion [Earthseed](/Earthseed) in [Octavia Butler](/Octavia_Butler)'s two book series comprised of [The Parable of the Sower](/The_Parable_of_the_Sower) and [The Parable of the Talents](/The_Parable_of_the_Talents).  She is the daughter of a Baptist minister who teaches at the local university and serves the community in their walled-in neighborhood.  Lauren suffers from hyperempathy, a condition that causes her to share in the pleasure and pain she perceives in others.  Given the dystopian future the books are set in, there is a lot more pain than pleasure to be shared!  Lauren's condition stems from her mother's addiction to Paracetco, a prescription drug she was abusing during her pregnancy.

As we follow Lauren through the two books she moves from being an isolated 18 year old to a revered religious leader for a religious movement that sends people out to seed the galaxy with life.  This transition is marked in the two books by a transition in how people refer to her.  In Parable of the Sower she is Lauren, in Parable of the Talents she is Olamina or "Shaper".
