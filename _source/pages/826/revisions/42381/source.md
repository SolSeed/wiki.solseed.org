John Wayne Potatoes

Heat oven to 350 degrees

Spray 9 x 13 pan with cooking spray.  

Line with tater tots (1 large bag)  

Sprinkle with 2 cups grated cheese.

Mix together in a bowl:       

6 eggs            1 cup milk      1/2 tsp seasoning salt (I use Lawrey’s and use about twice this amount)]

Pour over potatoes and cheese, easing egg through with a fork

Sprinkle top with 1/2 cup parmesan cheese and 4 chopped green onions

Bake 350 for 45 to 60 minutes (check middle of pan with fork to see if done)
