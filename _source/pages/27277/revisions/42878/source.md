#### Passionate
Passionate: [C-hands move down the chest repeatedly](http://www.signingsavvy.com/sign/PASSIONATE/2053/1)

#### Self Love
Self: [A hand taps chest repeatedly](http://www.signingsavvy.com/search/self)

Love: [Both arms are drawn to the body and cross over in front of the chest.](http://www.signingsavvy.com/sign/LOVE/255/1)

Self-Love: Tap chest with A hand twice (as in Self) and with both A hands once (as in love).
