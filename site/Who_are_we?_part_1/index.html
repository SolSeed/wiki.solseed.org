This was a session at the August 2018 Resilient Communities Mini-Unconference.

Attendees

  • Ben (convener, note-taker)
  • Eric
  • Shawn
  • Judy

Discussion

Ben: We each bring different perspectives and interests

  • Brandon is interested in AI safety
  • Eric is interested in Dyson trees
  • Shawn and Ben are interested in preserving knowledge through collapse
  • Ben is interested in building community that can survive collapse
  • Shelley is interested in making the group good for learning and developing children

Judy: Ribs of the umbrella we can feel good about containing us

  • Kicking around philosophical ideas
  • Eric: I used to call us a philosophy discussion group
  • Shawn: I've done a lot of thinking and wondering, and I'm now more action-oriented, although I was attracted to SolSeed because you were meeting and talking about the same idea I'd just developed
  • Desire to grow new living worlds
  • Some of us originally thought we would be the generation and the people to start creating new living worlds
  • Eric: Brandon talked about setting an audacious goal
  • Shawn: How do you respond to not attaining audacious goals?
  • Eric: The services are our strength, providing inspiration for us to go do our projects
  • At least for me, buying Buckleberry and trying to get into unfarming
  • If we keep them central, it makes sense to call ourselves a religion

Judy: I'm not theistic, but there are Jewish traditions I practice that help me feel connected to a long chain of people

  • I keep watching SolSeed wondering if a community will develop; my desire for belonging is to a loving community, not a church
  • I don't see that kind of community arising in Arranmore because of the homeowner association rules against e.g. building tiny houses
  • I was quite startled by the transition from wanting to build community in Buckman
  • Eric: At our 2014 unconference at Lost Lake, it was obvious to me that each of us had progressed too far along different life paths to come together in intentional community
  • Ben: It wasn't clarified that we weren't attracted enough to building community until the unconference at Tryon Farm
  • I also connect to forests as cathedrals

Side point: How to move to Canada, e.g. Ben moving to a community at Buckleberry and his parents following

Shawn: I also don't have a religion-shaped hole, although I have spent a lot of time spiritually seeking something greater than myself, not wanting to be a nihilist

  • I finally connected with life as the Cosmos knowing itself, but I don't connect so much with the Gaia story/metaphor
  • I get more out of the business calls than the services, partly because the service calls are very tuned toward the kids
  • I see how it makes sense for them; the Roman Catholic church services I went to as a child were super boring
  • I do like the more adult-oriented readings
  • Eric and Judy: Even children's stories can be read at multiple levels
  • Will SolSeed mature with the Sanders children? Can we plan ahead for that?
  • Will they rebel against it?
  • How to attract people who don't have young children?
  • Judy: People who do tend to hang out together and not with people who don't

Ben: If SolSeed suddenly decided it wanted to be an artists' collective, I'd be good with that

  • Judy: You already support each other in doing creative work
  • Shawn: That kind of creative work (stories, pictures, etc) isn't really my thing
  • Eric: I want to create a book, maybe a graphic novel or a book with a few illustrations, of my Gaia prayers
  • Side note on how to draw the illustrations

Shawn: I feel a sense of urgency to create community that can survive collapse

  • Eric: I started feeling urgency in 2016
  • Ben: I feel urgency that sometimes makes me want SolSeed to do more activism
  • Shawn: I see a different path for problem-solving than activism: A resilient community can help many other people after the system collapses
  • Argument with Judy about whether we're also obligated to work to prevent collapse, or whether collapse is inevitable
  • Shawn: Going orthogonal to head-butting conflicts
  • Judy: Taking personal responsibility for working toward good in the world
  • E.g. helping create a market for electric cars
  • It feels like people who don't take responsibility, e.g. for struggling to change the political paradigm that gave us Trump, are putting that burden on my shoulders
  • Judy and Ben: The hopelessness of realizing that despite all efforts and progress, not enough people are struggling to keep things getting better, and in fact all the progress will be undone

Argument about how much violence and how much cooperation will happen after a collapse

  • Ben: Can a community be built in such a way that it defends itself somewhat but also encourages cooperation with people who might otherwise overrun it?
  • Eric: In many cases, the violent people won't even show up in your community
  • Shawn: People wanting to become part of a community instead of destroying it
