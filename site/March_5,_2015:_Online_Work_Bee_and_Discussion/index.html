Redefining SolSeed

  • Two friends of SolSeed: SolSeed is a good name, and it should be a movement rather than a church … the world has enough churches
  • Brandon is considering having both a movement and a church (the movement could still be called SolSeed)
  • Ben observes that we need more opinions from others
  • Eric points out that many people are uncomfortable with the idea of joining a religion, but would join a movement
  • Ben agrees, pointing out space enthusiasts generally fit this description
  • Eric: also atheists and maybe environmentalists
  • Ben: but if we also had an associated religion, would people leave the movement as soon as they found that out?
  • Brandon suggests we should consider having a plurality of religions (including none) that are consistent with the values and goals of SolSeed. They use different language and practices to get at the same thing.
  • Brandon: Having, for example, some strong Christian rhetoric that gets at the ideas of SolSeed
