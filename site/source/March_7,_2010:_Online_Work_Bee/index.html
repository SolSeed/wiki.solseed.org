#### Draft of trifold language
The word SolSeed means "the body of all life on Earth."

We believe that:
- All life is precious.
- The Destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.

What we're about:
- Healing, sustaining, and nurturing life on Earth.
- Becoming a society of "starfarers," people with the values and consciousness needed to fulfill the Destiny.
- Fulfilling the Destiny: Helping life spread out from Earth to plant the seeds of new living worlds throughout the Galaxy.

The story so far:
- SolSeed was partly inspired by the fictional Earthseed religion in Octavia Butler's two *Parable* novels.
- The wiki site was established in February 2005, and is now hosted at solseed.org.
- We attracted members and friends in Oregon, Washington, Colorado, Illinois, Virginia, and New York.
- We held three major in-person gatherings in Oregon in 2009.

What we're doing now:
- Attending conventions like this one in search of like-minded people.
- Building a local community in Portland through monthly gatherings.
- Finishing up a music video and a children's book.

What we're planning:
- A bigger book and another video to introduce ourselves and our vision.
- A new wiki site to serve as a hub for contacts in the space industry.
- A SolSeed eco-village in east Portland.
