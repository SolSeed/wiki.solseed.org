The [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) has attracted people who have little use for a literal interpretation of [TheDestiny](/TheDestiny) or even for space travel as an inspiring story, but who feel drawn to participate by the group's strong [values](/values) and friendly atmosphere.  Others may find their interests align quite well, but simply need some time to learn more about [SolSeed](/SolSeed) and decide whether [becoming a member](/JoiningTheMovement) is right for them.  All these people can be defined as **Friends of SolSeed**, and belong to the much larger group of [Kindred](/Kindred) who share the basic SolSeed value of [bringing life](/bringing_life).

If you are a **Friend of SolSeed** who wants to be kept up to date on our doings every month via our [Newsletters](/Newsletters), please add your name with an email address or link to your userpage below.
