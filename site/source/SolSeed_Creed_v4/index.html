Our technology has led us to feel separate from the web of life,
Each of us trapped inside our conscious minds, yet masters of all we survey.

Living this story, we have done great harm to life,
Yet we have also created a wondrous explosion of new possibilities.
              
As dedicants of SolSeed, we are called to repay our debt to our shared body,
By using our technological prowess to help her give birth to a family of living worlds.      

I pledge to stoke the fires of my passion,
To have empathy for the parts of myself whose passions are sometimes unwise,
To use wisdom to channel my passion toward my highest aspirations.

I pledge to cultivate empathy for all living beings,
To grow my passion for helping others and working together,
To wisely choose paths that create more possibilities for all.

I pledge to constantly improve my wisdom,                                
To passionately strive toward greater knowledge of the world and more skillful living,
To let empathy drive me to share my wisdom with others.
