### Shawn's spiritual experience with 5-MEO-DMT
- Shawn's disclaimer: I take full responsibility for my actions
- The most important thing about any psychedelic experience is having the right setting and mindset going in, hence the rituals held by the group … it's otherwise not really religious
  - The rituals felt very Pagan and kind of playful
    - They talk a lot about the classical Greek four elements symbology, but also Hindu and Buddhist … it doesn't really matter which tradition(s) are referenced
  - The mat you sit on during the experience is called the Launching Pad
  - After inhaling the vaporized chemical, you lie back and blow it out
  - They play music that imitates beta, alpha, and delta waves … they gave me a CD of it
- What I felt
  - The experience of my ego dissolving felt like dying, but I told myself to surrender
    - Part of the purpose of these experiences is to "practice death"
  - Experiencing the world without the process of dividing it up, knowing what's right and wrong, etc
  - I felt peace, joy, love for everyone, everything made sense, but also I knew that I knew nothing
- I found clarity that my message is important, and that we need focused action without striving to create something beautiful and lasting
  - Similar to Buddhist insights about meditation or Kung Fu insights about how to do a move: the more you try to force yourself into it, the less it works, because your energy goes into the tension of forcing yourself … instead, relax into it
- The experience lasted about 25 minutes
- Having had this experience, I'm now considered an initiated member
- I don't expect people who haven't had this experience to fully understand what I'm talking about
  - I can forward you the materials on it, for what it's worth
