### March 24, 2009 @8pm PST
- Opening Words - 5 mins
- Check in - 10 mins
- What connects us all? - 30 mins
- Logistics / What's next / homework / assign next call - 10 mins
- Closing Words - 5 mins

The Destiny: A Reader's Theatre

*Brenna:* The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
  
*Stacey:* I don't get that. What does that mean?
  
*Brenna:* Well, destiny is kind of what's going to happen . . . and what we *want* to happen. 

*Brandon:* The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
  
*Ben:* So, that means spaceships, right?
  
*Brandon:* Well, it does for some. 

*Shelley:* The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
  
*Mark:* That sounds like it's important to you. Tell me more.
  
*Shelley:* Well, I do like the idea of embracing our shared present and future together -- our destiny.

*Boni:* The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
  
*Bob:* Say more.
  
*Boni:* I need to think about it a little more. The destiny means different things for every person. 

*Mark:* So, what you guys are saying is . . .
  
*Stacey:* They're saying that the destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
  
*Mark:* Maybe we need to talk a little more.
