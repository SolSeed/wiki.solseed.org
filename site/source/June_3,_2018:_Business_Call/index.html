### Gaia's People
Eric … I love the Gaia's People experiment
- I like how it makes me care more deeply/viscerally about environmental issues
  - Ben … I think I already had that oddly visceral reaction to global-scale problems
- I really love writing the [Gaia prayers](/SolSeed_Book_of_Days), and it bothers me that Shawn isn't attracted to the "mystical" exploration of the Gaia metaphor … then again, I'm not attracted to preparing for the apocalypse
  - Ben … I care about both

Eric … We haven't done much with it, e.g. rebranding the website
- Ben … We didn't rename the SolSeedCreed we're using, or even create a Gaia's People wiki page
  - But I have renamed the newsletter

Ben ... I like the Creed revision because I like using Gaia instead of SolSeed to mean "the body of all life" (the latter always seemed a bit arrogant to me)...but on the other hand I feel weird about "dedicating ourselves to Gaia" given that she's just a metaphor
- Eric … We're dedicating ourselves to the real thing that Gaia is a metaphor for
- Ben … In general, I could go either way on the name from a personal perspective

Ben … I'm wanting to try to recruit people who are really into science, and I can see the more Pagan feel of Gaia's People being a problem for that
- Eric … I haven't noticed a change in my word-of-mouth recruiting efforts … they didn't work before the name change and they still don't work now … I've never met anyone who was really interested
  - I do still have to point them to the SolSeed website/wiki, which is confusing after calling the group Gaia's People
- Ben … maybe I want to go back to looking for another name altogether
  - Eric … we didn't have much luck last time we tried that
