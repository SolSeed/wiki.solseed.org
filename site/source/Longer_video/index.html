We would like to create a video somewhere around 15 minutes long to introduce the [SolSeed Movement](/SolSeed_Movement) more clearly and explicitly than the [Pick a Future](/Pick_a_Future) music video does.

#### Working title
**SolSeed: Bring Life!**

#### Draft script
*Begin with a brief series of closeups on our main speakers.*

BRANDON
The SolSeed Movement is about bringing life: protecting it, nurturing it, helping it thrive and grow.

MARK, MEG, or LYRIC
It's about bringing life to ourselves and each other...

SHELLEY
To our families and communities...

BEN
To the living world we call home...

BRANDON
And to other worlds across the galaxy.

*Dissolve to the galaxy with green flares and the title* 

LIVING WORLD PRODUCTIONS presents...

*This fades as we pan down to follow a spiral arm that transitions to the familiar band of stars.  A night-side Earth begins to come into frame.  Sol rises from behind it, spreading a crescent of light along its limb.  This extends to form the [SolSeed emblem](/Media:WebIntroGraphic.png) as the rest of Earth comes into view.  Then another title in orange characters fades in below the word SolSeed:*

Bring Life!

*This title shrinks and moves down toward Sol, which is still growing in brightness.*

*Dissolve through white to a matching closeup on the "Bring Life!" logo on Brandon's SolSeed jacket.  Zoom out to a medium shot.*

BRANDON
Hi, I'm Brandon CS Sanders, founder of the SolSeed Movement.  I created this movement for people like me who want their lives to be part of an epic story--the story of SolSeed, the body of all life on Earth, and humanity, SolSeed's intelligent spark, whose destiny is to take life out into space and ultimately to seed the galaxy with life.
