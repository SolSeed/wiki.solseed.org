*This was a session at [Sol 2012](/Sol_2012).*

[Shelley](/Shelley): What do you most desire to accomplish in the next thirty years?

- [Eric](/Eric): Making trees grow in environments where they have never grown before, as precursor to [Dyson trees](/Dyson_trees) growing in outer space
- [Lion](/Lion): Developing my intentional society
  - Creating a School of Dream
  - Restoring erotic sexual love (need to develop language to describe this as it's a touchy subject)
  - Developing a model society that organizes politics, economics, discourse, etc. around Dream
- [Hank](/Hank): Having some of the kids I've taught contribute to a better world and the survival of humanity, as well as making some progress toward going to the stars
- [Ben](/Ben): Helping to make the following things happen:
  - My webcomic, *[Flight in a Cage](/Flight_in_a_Cage),* covering all my core interests
  - A game called *Wings* that would give you the experience of being a bird
  - A self-sustaining ecosystem in an airtight box, as precursor to a life-support system for space travel and colonization
- [Shelley](/Shelley):
  - Supporting my children in being who they are
  - Working to create better healthcare that leads to better health at lower cost
- [Brandon](/Brandon): Creating an annual weekend event where SolSeed brings an inspiring spectacle, and [other tribes](/Kindred) of very alive people also come to present themselves to people like [Sequoia](/Sequoia) who could choose among them or start their own tribe
