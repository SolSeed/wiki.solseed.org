### Empathy
[Empathy](/Empathy) (same as compassion): [Both hands using touch fingers, make circular forward stroking motions in the air with the dominate hand slightly behind the non-dominate hand.](http://www.signingsavvy.com/sign/EMPATHY/3367/1)
