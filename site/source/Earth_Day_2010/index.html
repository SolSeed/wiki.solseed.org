__NOTOC__

SolSeed had a big "booth" at the ["Re-Seeding Our World"](http://cityrepair.org/projects/earthday/) Earth Day event in east Portland, including two activities for passers-by to participate in: a mural painting with ideas for the [SolSeedVillage](/SolSeedVillage), and a galaxy-shaped planter where people could plant lettuce seeds (see ["Lettuce" Bring Life](/"Lettuce"_Bring_Life) for photos of the lettuce's growth).
