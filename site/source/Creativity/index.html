[Creativity](/Creativity) is the power to imagine a better future.
Without [Creativity](/Creativity),
we could only [Hope](/Hope) for stasis, a continuance of the way things are,
denying [Pragmatism](/Pragmatism), which teaches that [change](/change) is eternal.
[Perseverance](/Perseverance) in the mission of fighting Change always fails eventually.
*

Creativity may be the most powerful gift of intelligence.  And if we keep trying to apply the same tired old solutions to our rapidly mutating global problems, it's clear that we will fail miserably.  Yes, the forces of technological innovation got us into this mess.  They're also the only thing powerful enough to get us out of it in one piece.
