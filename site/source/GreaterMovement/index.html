There is a movement.  This is true whether or not we recognize and name it.  We choose to name it the [GreaterMovement](/GreaterMovement) so that we can consciously support it.

We are each composed of and shape the flows around us.  This is life.  We live.  Our universe lives.

Sometimes flows [BringLife](/BringLife) and sometimes they [DrainLife](/DrainLife).  We call the parts of ourselves and the universe that routinely [BringLife](/BringLife) "the [GreaterMovement](/GreaterMovement)".  Every individual and organization is sometimes growing the [GreaterMovement](/GreaterMovement) and sometimes sapping it.

We name as [Kindred](/Kindred) those individuals and organizations whose wise actions embody the values and principles of the [GreaterMovement](/GreaterMovement) and inspire us to deepen our practice.
