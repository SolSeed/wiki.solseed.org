### [Kindred2011](/Kindred2011) Invitation
You are receiving this invitation for [Kindred2011](/Kindred2011) because someone finds you inspiring and wants to share the experience of [Kindred2011](/Kindred2011) with you.  [Kindred](/Kindred) are those who are called to [Thrive](/Thrive) and [Kindred2010](/Kindred2010) is their festival celebrating [Life](/Life)!  Like individual cells working in the pattern of a human body, [Kindred](/Kindred) work within the pattern of [The Body of All Life](/The_Body_of_All_Life).  While our action is often local and specific, our sphere of care encompasses the entire [Body of All Life](/Body_of_All_Life).

At [Kindred2011](/Kindred2011) we come together for a festival of sharing.  As we share, the enthusiasm of others re-energizes us. As we share, we weave together the threads of wisdom that each individual and each organization brings.  As we share, we experience the love and community of a [Greater Movement](/Greater_Movement).

Please add your name to the [list of those planning to attend Kindred2011](/Kindred2011/Participants).  Then pass along this invitation to others that you want to share the experience with.  This event  gets its life from the life that we each bring!
