We're planning to raise $10,000 to bring clean drinking water to a village in the Tigray region of Ethiopia.  We're partnering with [Water Partners International](http://water.org).  For now, we're using a donation site that can only send general donations to support all of WPI's projects worldwide, but we're interested in making it more local and personal as soon as the technology becomes available.  Please poke around [on their website](http://water.org) for more information.

View these two files for details about the Tigray region and what a water source can mean for people living there.
