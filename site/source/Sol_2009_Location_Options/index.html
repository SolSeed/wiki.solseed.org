### Large Group Options for [Sol 2009](/Sol_2009)
Oceanside is our first choice, but another coastal town would be fine too.

I think we're aiming for 30 people.

We need a large meeting space and an oceanfront view.

Some of us could camp nearby.

Options:
- [End of the Continent](http://www.oregonbeachvacations.com/rental/house.html?ID=289&User=ORB9044&Avail=&Stay=) sleeps 22 at Gleneden Beach. My favorite in terms of how nice the house is.
- [Whale Watcher](http://www.oregonbeachvacations.com/rental/house.html?ID=287&User=ORB9044&Avail=&Stay=) sleeps 20 in Lincoln City. Could work.
- [Captain John's Beach House](http://www.oregonbeachvacations.com/rental/house.html?ID=134&User=ORB9044&Avail=&Stay=) sleeps 18 at Lincoln City.
- [Shear Water](http://www.oregonbeachvacations.com/rental/house.html?ID=140&User=ORB9044&Avail=&Stay=) sleeps 16 at Depoe Bay
- [Penthouse Escape](http://www.oregonbeachvacations.com/rental/house.html?ID=126&User=ORB9044&Avail=&Stay=) sleeps 17 in Lincoln City. "Ocean view" but 2 blocks to beach.
