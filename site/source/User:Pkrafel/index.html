I am interested in nourishing upward spirals. I made a DVD titled *[The Upward Spiral](/The_Upward_Spiral)* which some of you have seen. I go out into the rain to play with runoff; water has been a good teacher for me. I also wrote a book, *Seeing Nature*. 

My wife, Alysia, and I founded Chrysalis, a small chartered public school with a focus on science and nature. The school has become a wonderful community for teachers, students, and parents. 

I'm wanting to share some of my latest thoughts from roaming the fields and explore possible connections with the work of others.
