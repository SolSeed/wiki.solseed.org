Websites we admire from organizations with similar tactical goals

  • Nova ... clean, nice carousel, reflows perfectly from large to mobile (built with html5boilerplate, Foundation, and jQuery)
  • MercyCorps ... clean, nice carousel, reflows perfectly from large to mobile (built with Drupal CMS and jQuery)
  • American Museum of Natural History ... clean, nice carousel, reflows perfectly from large to mobile (built with eZ Systems CMS and jQuery (JCarousel)
  • World Wildlife Foundation ... clean (amazing pictures), reflows perfectly from large to mobile (built with rails, jQuery, scriptaculous, etc)
  • Bella Gaia if it reflowed cleanly it would probably be my favorite ... it emphasizes their trailer very well, very little clutter around it, yet it still has social media buttons and a significant menu system, nice carousel on subpages ... ([built with Weebly drag and drop CMS, FancyBox for carousel, jQuery, vimeo rather than YouTube for video)
  • https://www.ikat.org/ Central Asia Institute ... I primarily appreciate their effective use of pictures (simpler site uses a slideshow rather than a carousel)
  • Oregon Humane Society and Vladimir Kush for the effective use of art rather than photos

Almost all of these are using HTML5
