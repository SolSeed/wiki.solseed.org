We held our second annual Longest Night Festival on the evening of Tuesday, December 21, 2010, continuing the tradition of the previous year's [Solstice party](/Solstice2009#Longest_Night_Festival_Party_Invitation) and our other Solstice and Equinox celebrations. Our core community and family, friends, and neighbors all showed up at the Seed Village to celebrate the Solstice night with fire, food, and song. Here are some photos from the event:
