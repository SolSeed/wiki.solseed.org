The eight liturgical seasons of the SolSeed Movement, each with its own ritual used during the [Weekly Service Calls](/Weekly_Service_Calls), represent a special kind of [Cosmic Calendar](/SolSeed_Cosmic_Calendar).  Each season culminates in a [Celebration](/Celebration).
- 1 - [Genesis](/Genesis) Winter Solstice to Mid Winter
- 2 - [Conflatorium](/Conflatorium) Mid Winter to Spring Equinox
- 3 - [Viriditas de Terra](/Viriditas_de_Terra) Spring Equinox to Mid Spring
- 4 - [Diversitas](/Diversitas) Mid Spring to Summer Solstice
- 5 - [Sophos](/Sophos) Summer Solstice to Mid Summer
- 6 - [Civis](/Civis) Mid Summer to Fall Equinox
- 7 - [Viventibus Mundos](/Viventibus_Mundos) Fall Equinox to Mid Fall
- 8 - [Viventibus Galaxia](/Viventibus_Galaxia) Mid Fall to Winter Solstice
