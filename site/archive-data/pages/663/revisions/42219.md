[The Body of All Life](/The_Body_of_All_Life) is a lot like a human body.  If you think about your own body, there are billions of cells who don't know who you are, and really don't care about you.  And yet, just by all those cells fulfilling their role within the pattern of your body, "you" emerge!  How cool is that!  Most of the plants and animals that make up [The Body of All Life](/The_Body_of_All_Life) also don't know who it is, and really don't care about it.  And yet, just by all those plants and animals fulfilling their role within the pattern of life, amazing possibilities emerge and are made manifest!

[The Body of All Life](/The_Body_of_All_Life) has many names: [Gaia](/Gaia), [Mother Nature](/Mother_Nature), and [SolSeed](/SolSeed) are just a few of those names.

Just like the cells in your body care for your body, and your body in turn cares for your cells, so too can we care for [The Body of All Life](/The_Body_of_All_Life). If we do our part to provide and care for the body of all life, it will provide and care for us.
