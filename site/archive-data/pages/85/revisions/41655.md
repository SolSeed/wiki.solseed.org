### Is SolSeed a religion?
[SolSeed](/SolSeed) is currently reconsidering whether or not to [organize](/Legal_Organization) as a religion.  We're certainly not a traditional religion, given that we embrace [science](/science) and don't believe in gods or supernatural forces, but we are also embracing the power of [religious ritual](/SolSeedServices) as an effective psychological tool to motivate ourselves.  This ritual helps us be happier through feeling connected to others and to a cause that's larger than ourselves, and regularly motivates us to meet our commitments.  We also consider "reverence for [life](/life)" as a core tenet, while looking forward to the spiritual [Destiny](/Destiny) of acting as seeds to spread life to other worlds.

Here is a [dialogue](http://www.xtranormal.com/watch/11077454/) that would help explain why we chose to become a religion.  We use a metaphor to explain what kind of religion we are and avoid mistaken assumptions: "SolSeed is a branch of the [Church of Science](/Being_a_Starfarer#Truth,_Beauty,_and_Values_(the_religion_of_science))."

### Can I join the [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) and keep my existing religion?
Regardless of the above decision, SolSeed makes no statements about God or the supernatural, other than that we, the founding members ([Brandon](/Brandon), [Shelley](/Shelley), and [Ben](/Ben)), *don't* believe in such things.  In looking for new [members](/members), we don't discriminate on the basis of religion (though we do discourage [magical thinking](/magical_thinking)), and we think the ideals and goals of [SolSeed](/SolSeed) ought to be integratable without conflict into any kind of faith.
