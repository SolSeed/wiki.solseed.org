[Perseverance](/Perseverance) matches long-term actions to long-term thinking.
Without [Perseverance](/Perseverance),
we would [Hope](/Hope) for change but never succeed in making it happen,
no matter how much [Creativity](/Creativity) and [Pragmatism](/Pragmatism) we applied to the problem.
*

When [Octavia Butler](/Octavia_Butler) came to speak at Pomona College in the fall of 2002, she told us that if we want to write novels, we don't really need any talent but the willingness to persevere.  Writing a novel takes a massive commitment of time and effort, and the biggest danger is simply that you will give up in the middle.

How much harder, then, will it be to avoid giving up in the middle of a centuries-long [project](/TheDestiny) to heal our world and plant the [Seeds of Gaia](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGqu8miuMNI) on other worlds?
