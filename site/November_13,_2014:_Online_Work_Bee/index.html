Outreach

Interstellar

  • On the one hand, this movie raises awareness about the idea of interstellar travel and space colonies being a good thing
  • Are we seeing a tipping point in popular consciousness about space? If so, why, and what does the zeitgeist look like?
  • On the other hand, it's all about fleeing to some other planet after this one gets broken
  • So let's talk about other possible motivations/scenarios
  • Human-centric vs. Life-centric
  • Rosetta and Philae, Dawn (Ceres flyby in April 2015)

Kindred Map

Brandon wants to make a conceptual map of kindred groups and how they intersect

  • People could participate by making rank-ordered lists of values, and finding people and groups with similar lists
  • You can also say what you think another group or person's values are (to explain why you feel aligned with them)
  • Start with a spreadsheet, then build up to a graphical thing like a Venn diagram
  • Brandon has more energy for this than for Kindred 2015 because he wants to get to know all these new contacts before inviting them to fly across the country

Eric observes that we might find a need to focus on one nexus of interest at a time, e.g. naturalistic religion (which has very little overlap with space)

  • Maybe we could push the Spiritual Naturalist Society or somebody into organizing a Kindred event?
  • Brandon doesn't have much interest in events that just target one cluster at a time, but admits that an event with us as the intersection probably isn't viable yet
