Where:

  • Portland
  • Pros: Convenient for the most people, allowing us to have a bigger event.
  • Cons: Eric might not want to come even though he loves Portland.
  • Windward
  • Pros: Like Wow! Awesome place to go because it's been successful at being the kind of community we want to talk about for 40 years. The stars there are amazing. We could do YayLifeTri and Drumming the Eons at Maryhill/Stonehenge. Shawn is already in touch with them around possibly getting an internship there.
  • Cons: Out of the way, might be expensive, and we need to get permission.
  • Ottawa area
  • Pros: Buckleberry (visit Gaia statue, help build stuff), Frontenac North Earthship Village, Lanark Eco Village.
  • Cons: Only close for Eric; far for everyone else. (Except maybe some East Coast Earthseeders)
  • Seattle
  • Pros: People from Star Community might come, and maybe even provide a venue
  • Cons: Do we want to do two Seattle events in a row? Ben wants to come to Portland in May to visit family
  • Vancouver or Victoria, B.C.
  • Pros: Easy for Portland and Seattle people to get to, doesn't break Eric's vow
  • Cons: Americans would have to have passports?
  • Humboldt Redwoods State Park (roughly the location of Acorn)
  • Pros: Beautiful, roughly where Acorn is in the Earthseed books (very attractive for Earthseed people), Eric is willing to travel to the U.S. for it; Like Wow.
  • Cons: A long way from anywhere, we'd need to reserve campsites right now
  • Brandon's proposal: Try to do a sustainable/resilient communities unconference at Windward in May, and target a festival celebrating Life in the redwoods in August
