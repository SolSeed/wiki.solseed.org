Holacracy (20 min)

How to select/elect the Lead Link

  • The Lead Link in the anchor circle (Stewards) comes from some mechanism outside of the holacracy (in our case consensus). The Lead Link for a circle cannot also be the facilitator for that circle.
  • Holacracy assumes a hierarchical organization by default; the boss becomes the Lead Link.
  • Where do roles come from? Can anyone in a circle propose a role?
  • How are roles changed?
  • How are roles filled/staffed?
  • Brandon thinks only the Lead Link can appoint people to non-elected roles in a circle
