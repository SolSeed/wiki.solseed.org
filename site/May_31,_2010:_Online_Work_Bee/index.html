SpaceWiki and New Space Exhibition

  • Booth people don't need admission passes
  • But we can get one comped and use it to go to a few sessions
  • Going ahead with this!
  • Get the new website skin & some caricatures up first, then hit people up for sponsorships
  • Should we say "SpaceWiki is a project of the SolSeed Movement?"
  • Maybe hidden away on an About Us page
  • Main page should be "why does this exist?"
  • For newcomers who want to get involved in space-related fields, it's daunting to find the right entry point
  • We want to help people understand what the problem space of space-industry networking is like and get people up to speed quickly
  • Also, serve as useful outlet for people within the community to collect interesting, colorful things, personal stories about the people in the companies rather than PR or objective reporting about the companies themselves
  • Why wiki? Grassroots, bottom-up way to create, creates another example of space enthusiasts self-organizing
