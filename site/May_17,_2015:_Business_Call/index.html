Spectacle

Ben has been to 3 science museums that have an exhibit called Science on a Sphere; a sphere suspended from the ceiling with four projectors projecting presentations on it. We have one projector and could maybe get someone like Bob Sanders who likes to make things to make a hemispherical screen, and we could project distorted images onto the hemisphere that would then become undistorted by the counter distortion of the sphere. We could show things like Earth's bioregions and Earth in visible and infrared light and maybe Mars
