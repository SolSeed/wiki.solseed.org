Octavia Estelle Butler (June 22, 1947 — February 24, 2006) was the author of the [Earthseed](/Earthseed) series "The Parable of the Sower" and "The Parable of the Talents".  These two books follow Lauren Olamina as she develops [Earthseed](/Earthseed), a humanist religion dedicated to helping people help themselves.  Lauren is a young African American women trying to incubate [Earthseed](/Earthseed) in the midst of the chaos of a crumbling society.  The two [Earthseed](/Earthseed) "Parables" books inspired the creation of the real-world [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement).
