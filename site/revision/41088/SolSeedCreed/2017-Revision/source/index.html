Here is [[Eric]]'s new draft version of the [[SolSeed Creed]]:

We are SolSeed
: We are the Body of all Earthly Life

Our Four Holy Tools are
: Knowledge of the Past,
: Mindfulness of the Present,
: Hope-filled Visions of the Future and
: Practices that Increase our Skill with the other three

With these tools
: We create our lives
:: Lives of understanding, love and hope

The past informs us.
: Without knowledge of the past,
:: Mindfulness, vision and practice are misguided

I pledge to study the history of
: Life exuberant,
: Ever Bursting through boundaries to flower and spread,
: Ever Creating the conditions for more Life
:: in an upward spiral of exponentiating possibilities

The present grounds us
: Without mindfulness of the present,
:: Knowledge frightens us, 
:: our vision is one of despair, 
:: and our practices serve only to distract us.

I pledge to always be mindful of the fact
: That Life is precious
: That Life has always been and will always be
:: in a state of being precious

The future inspires us
: Without visions of the future
:: Knowledge, mindfulness and practice stagnate

I pledge 
: To express the excellent nature of Gaia
: To strive toward the Great Birthing
:: In which SolSeed takes root and flowers among the stars and
:: Gaia gives birth to a family of living worlds

Practice teaches us
: Without practice
:: Knowledge and vision dim, and mindfulness escapes us

I pledge to answer Gaia's call
: To Dedicate myself to Life
: To join my kindred
: In a community of practice
: To align our words, thoughts, actions, habits, characters and very beings
: With our Highest Aspirations

I pledge to use art and the natural world
: To awaken in myself and others
:: That Cosmic Religious Feeling
:: That ignites wonder
:: Fosters compassion
:: And Inspires Invention

Our Three Sacred Duties are to 
: Embrace Passion
: Cultivate Empathy and
: Pursue Wisdom

So that
: Our Teaching Spreads Truth
: Our Loving Engenders Kindness and 
: Our Striving Hastens the Great Birthing

''Passion call and response unchanged, Empathy call unchanged''

I pledge to carry in my heart
: the fact that as I am alive, you are alive,
: And in kinship will all other living beings
I pledge to love others as I love myself
: To treat their needs as if they were my own
: To Grow ours, Not just get mine.

''Wisdom call unchanged''

I pledge 
: To stand ready to throw out my beliefs 
:: In the face of new evidence 
: To experiment with new practices
:: Which engender mindfulness
: To explore new more hopefilled visions of the future

With 
: Knowledge, Mindfulness, Vision and Practice and
Through
: Passion, Empathy and Wisdom
We have come to know that...

''Final response unchanged''
