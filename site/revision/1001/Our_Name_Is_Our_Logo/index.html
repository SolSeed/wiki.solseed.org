<table border="1"> <tr> <td>'''Description'''</td> <td>'''Image''' (click for full size)</td> <td>'''On the surface'''</td> <td>'''Closer look'''</td> <td>'''Deeper meaning'''</td> </tr> <tr> This logo/emblem design was originally a variant on the Earth-as-a-tree concept that emphasizes our rootedness in energy from [[Sol]], thus helping to explain the term [[SolSeed]]. It now accomplishes much more. For one thing, it could resolve the [[June 2, 2009: Flower and Roots Metaphor#Topic 2: Roots and Flowers (15_mins)|debate]] about metaphors for members of the [[SolSeedMovement]] who prefer to focus on literal vs. metaphorical interpretations of [[TheDestiny]]: the latter represent the "[[Sol]] aspect" of the movement, while the former represent the "[[Seed]] aspect." (And of course some people are in between.) <td valign="top"> Basic emblem design, suitable for many uses and many media (ink, thread, wire, etc.) </td> <td valign="top"> [[Image:Logo-simple.gif|150px]] </td> <td valign="top"> * The O is a symbolic [[Sol]] with four rays. * The d represents a maple [[seed]]. </td> <td valign="top"> * The S is shaped roughly like a yin-yang symbol and could also be viewed as [http://peacebuttons.info/E-News/ecologysymbol.htm the ecology symbol, theta]. * The two e's trace the spiral path of a whirling maple seed. </td> <td> * As noted above, the two words represent two intertwined aspects of the [[SolSeedMovement]]: ** ''SOL'' represents [[SolSeedCommunity|community]], sustainability, centeredness, a focus on the present. It might be defined as the "feminine principle." ** ''Seed'' represents outward motion, progress, [[change]], a focus on the future. It might be defined as the "masculine principle" (the maple seed is even kind of phallic). ** Compare the logo of the advocacy group [http://equalitynow.org Equality Now]: [[Image:EqualityNow.png|24px]] </td> </tr> </table>
