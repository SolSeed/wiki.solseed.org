This was a session at the August 2018 Resilient Communities Mini-Unconference.

Attendees

  • Brandon (convener)
  • Shelley
  • Ben (note-taker)
  • Eric
  • Shawn

Discussion

Brandon: We're the people who want to steer this group and be stewards of it

  • Ben: Are we all a 5 on that?
  • Everyone says yes

Shelley: I appreciate SolSeed as a way to bring structure and validation to a core set of practices for our family, particularly the kids.

  • The services aren't meeting the children's needs because they're geared too much toward adults, so trying to do both isn't working
  • Brandon and I have been discussing having an in-person practice in nature, maybe at the Marquam Shelter, with plays and stories and songs
  • Brandon: We're looking to put time and energy into developing a curriculum for our kids
  • Shelley: We'll keep testing the Gaia prayers as part of that curriculum
  • The structure of the year with the services is foreign to me; we might only participate in those once a month

Brandon: We need to create space to put our children's needs first, without even considering other people's needs for a while

  • We're not trying to recruit other families
  • I won't come to the business calls anymore
  • Ben: In that case, it will be hard to think of you as part of SolSeed!
  • Eric: I will really miss you and your kids; as with my wife moving to Eganville, I'm feeling a loss of connection
  • Brandon: Metaphor of SolSeed as a family: if someone goes away to pursue an opportunity, it doesn't mean they're not part of the family
  • Talking about things like politics and collapse in front of my kids isn't creating the environment they need to thrive
  • Shawn: It's hard for me not to feel somewhat responsible, considering your cohesion is breaking down just after I showed up
  • Shelley and Brandon: No, it's driven by a long history, Brandon's illness, and Shelley's increased willingness to speak up for her needs and the kids' needs
  • Ben: Are you also quitting the Concerning AI podcast?
  • Brandon: I don't know; it's the only time I see my friend Ted

Ben: Two branches that are different types of group?

  • Brandon: I still think of it as one body; I fit what we're doing for our kids into my narrative of SolSeed
  • Eric: We've been working on separate projects for a while
  • But who comes makes a difference to what happens, e.g. I've been kid-oriented with some of my SolSeed activities
  • Brandon: Each branch will be exploring the question “Who are we?” and I look forward to sharing the answers we find
  • Shawn: If we view this as a semi-collapse of SolSeed, that creates an opportunity for new things to grow (just as how I'm optimistic about civilizational collapse)
