The [[SolSeedVillage]] is a planned eco-village to be built somewhere in East Portland, Oregon. In rough outline, it will be a cohousing community with some dorm-style living for volunteers, substantial food-producing gardens, and workshops including spaces dedicated to one or more space-related industries. Sustainability and green building techniques will be central to its design. == Visualization for Video == [[Ben]] is making a few brief shots of one concept for what the [[SolSeedVillage]] might one day look like, for use in his video [[Pick a Future]] and possibly for banners to be displayed at events such as the OryCon science fiction convention.
