Key:
{| border=1
|-
| 0 - embryonic || the tribe is an idea but does not yet have an online hub
|-
| 1 - virtual || the tribe has begun to coalesce, but is all online with no significant face-to-face yet
|-
| 2 - convened || the tribe has at least one regular yearly face-to-face get together
|-
| 3 - regular || the tribe has at least monthly face-to-face get togethers (in person or via videoconferencing)
|-
| 4 - in situ || the tribe has its own dedicated physical meeting place or places
|-
| 5 - established and recognized || lots of people in the broader community know of them and interact with them
|-
| 6 - time-tested || the tribe has been fully established and active for at least a decade
|}
