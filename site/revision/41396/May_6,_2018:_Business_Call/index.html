== Gaia's People == * Shawn: It sounds more mystical and religious that my impression of what this group is about … I'm much more of a secular person, and in my mind the mystical aspects don't serve much … my preference is for a more academic approach ** Ben: That's a new idea for us … we'd previously thought about someday having a community with a school for children, but that's all ** Shawn: The ceremonies we do seem to be geared toward children … the readings Brandon and Shelley do seem centered around their kids … will the ceremonies change as they grow up, or are we going to stay kid-focused? * Shawn: Is this a name for us to identify with, or to speak to other people? ** Ben: Both … I share your concern about mysticism and the name attracting a bunch of mystically-minded people, but I also really like the concept of Gaia ** Shawn: I don't identify with it myself … it makes sense as a metaphor, but the more we personify Gaia as a conscious being, the more we're likely to attract more mystically-minded people and fewer scientifically-minded people *** Ben: Brandon and Eric would say the purpose of personification is to activate a part of our brains that gains something from telling that kind of story … Brandon calls it the "religion-shaped hole" in our minds *** Shawn: I may have a problem with that part of my brain … I think people need a sense of purpose/reason for being, but not a deity *** Ben: The tree of life is what gave birth to us… the thing that might be Gaia is both what gave birth to us and something we're part of … I'm dubious about personification/deity, but all in on having awe and reverence (i.e. religious feelings) toward Earth and the Universe *** Shawn: For me, life (and consciousness) is what's special … the particular tree of life on Earth isn't as significant, and deifying it as Gaia feels like Earth chauvinism, akin to creating a deity for the United States *** Shawn: The metaphors seem more useful when talking to children who don't know about entropy, biochemistry, etc *** Ben: There is a difference between the idea of Earth as an entity to be revered and the United States … Earth is a very isolated instance of life with clear physical boundaries (as well as being the only instance we currently know), whereas the U.S. is just human-created arbitrary lines on a map (e.g. the Cascadia bioregion cuts right through the border) **** Shawn: Would you personify Cascadia as a deity?
