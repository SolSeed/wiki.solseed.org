[[Seed2009]] is a retreat and [[OpenSpace]] event hosted by members and friends of the [[SolSeedMovement]]. The theme question for the event is: '''What does it mean to "Bring Life" to each of us, to all of us, and to the entire galaxy?''' What gives you life? What gives life to the communities you care about? How can we notice this life-giving in our daily lives and create the conditions for more of that to happen? If you are planning to attend add yourself to [[Seed_2009/Participants|the participants list]]. == Where == [[FalconsCrestLodge]] on Mount Hood, about 1 hour from Portland. The lodge sleeps 24, provides numerous cozy spaces for [[OpenSpace]] sessions, and has a satellite Internet connection. == When == January 9, 10, and a half day on January 11, 2009. We have [[FalconsCrestLodge|the lodge]] from 3 pm Thursday, January 8 through 11am Monday morning January 12. For more details see [[Seed2009/Schedule|the schedule]]. == Cost == Free!
