Seeking to have a full suite of practices.

Have: 
* Daily individual mindfulness practice (meditation)
* Work bees
* Small Group "Service" Discussions
* Seasonal Celebrations

Missing:
* Shared understanding of what a practice is
* compassion (for weak, for earth)
* simplicity
* digging in the soil
* making things that bring life (SpaceHacking, DIY ecology, robots, kites and balloons that map things, etc)
* Congregational Size "Being Together" Meetings
* ecological sacrifice (celebration and support, space in our lives)
* kindred festivals

For Discussion:
* Blessings for meals
* Tithing
* Sabbath
