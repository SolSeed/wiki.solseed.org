Opportunity Profile

Opportunity Sketch

Attract people to SolSeed by attracting them first to the core principle: life is precious, specifically by convincing them that trees are precious and then getting them to volunteer with a movement to care for trees.

Outcomes

  • Trees are cared for and become more plentiful that they otherwise would be.
  • Growth of a kindred non-speciest movement that shares our core values; 'life is precious' and 'more life is more precious'.

Impact

  • More life.
  • More kindred.

Cost

$1000

1000's hrs

Steps

  • Find and join groups that care about local trees
  • Organise committees/projects within these groups that take care of local trees
  • Maybe use pedal-powered water pumps to water trees.
