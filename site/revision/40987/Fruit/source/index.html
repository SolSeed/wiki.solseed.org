The [[Fruit]]s of the [[SolSeedMovement]] are embodied in the character that comes from our practice.

We use our [[Practice]] to align our thoughts, words, actions, habits, character ... our very being with our highest aspirations.

* [[Bringing life to ourselves]]
** Love ... undefeatable benevolence and unconquerable goodwill, that always seeks the highest of the other, no matter what s/he does
** Joy ... serene satisfaction that comes from being a part of and contributing to a larger whole
** Peace ... being fundamentally okay with Life as it is and a lived equanimity with Life's ups and downs
** Patience ... willingness to bear wrongs patiently
** Kindness ... acting for the welfare of those who are taxing our patience
** Goodness ... being our best selves, being worthy of our calling, being worthy of the unbroken thread that led to us.  In the words of Carl Sagan, Our obligation to survive and flourish is owed not just to ourselves but also to that cosmos ancient and vast from which we spring!
** Faithfulness ... personal resolve to not wander from our commitments and purpose
** Gentleness ... 
** Self-control ... 

* [[Bringing life to the Solar System]]
** The Exuberant Life story becomes widespread and displaces the consumption story
*** Intentional communities
*** etc
** Life spreads throughout the Solar System
