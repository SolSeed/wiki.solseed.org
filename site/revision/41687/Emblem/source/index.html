[SolSeed](/SolSeed) < [SolSeed Emblem](/SolSeed_Emblem)

There is no official Emblem for SolSeed, but several have seen fairly wide use.  With the most prevalent being some form the the sunrise rocket flower bud (it needs a better name) shown below.  Read on for more of the history of where this came from.

### Current
This is an "earth as seed" emblem, with an explicit yin and yang, plus an obvious womb shape.  Note also the three leaves ... for [Passion](/Passion), [Wisdom](/Wisdom), and [Empathy](/Empathy) and the rocket shaped flower.  It also evokes the upward spiral.  The yin-yang shape is also a giant S for SolSeed.

![400px](/assets/uploads/SolSeed-Emblem.png)

- Yet another alternate emblem concept by [Ben](/User:BenSibelman) (really too complex to be an emblem, but still a nice symbolic graphic).  The one above treats the Earth as a sprouting seed.  This one reverses the metaphor and looks at spaceships, going out to plant and grow new living worlds, as the seeds.
