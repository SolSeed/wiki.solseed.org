SolSeed < SolSeed Emblem

There is no official Emblem for SolSeed, but several have seen fairly wide use. With the most prevalent being some form the the sunrise rocket flower bud (it needs a better name) shown below. Read on for more of the history of where this came from.

Current

This is an "earth as seed" emblem, with an explicit yin and yang, plus an obvious womb shape. Note also the three leaves ... for Passion, Wisdom, and Empathy and the rocket shaped flower. It also evokes the upward spiral. The yin-yang shape is also a giant S for SolSeed.

Historical

  • The original rocket-ship emblem created by Brandon.

  • Two concepts for alternate emblems created by Kevin:

  • A concept by Ben: Our Name Is Our Logo
  • Another emblem concept proposed by Ben (click the image for a description of the ideas that went into it). We used the simplified version as our "official" emblem for a few years.

  • Yet another alternate emblem concept by Ben (really too complex to be an emblem, but still a nice symbolic graphic). The one above treats the Earth as a sprouting seed. This one reverses the metaphor and looks at spaceships, going out to plant and grow new living worlds, as the seeds.

Current

  • The balanced emblem created by Brandon as a revision of Ben's "earth as seed" emblem, with more explicit yin and yang, plus an obvious womb shape. Note also the three leaves ... for Passion, Wisdom, and Empathy.

  • The balanced emblem thickened up and entirely connected for 3D printing
