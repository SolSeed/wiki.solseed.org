[Seed2009](/Seed2009) will be held at Falcon's Crest on Mount Hood 1 hour away from Portland Oregon.  The lodge sleeps 24 and has lots of cozy spaces for [OpenSpace](/OpenSpace) sessions.

See the website for the lodge at http://www.falconscrestlodge.com/

#### Sleeping Accomodations
The lodge sleeps 24 with 3 king master suites that have their own bathrooms and two bunk rooms that sleep 6 each and also have their own bathrooms.  It also looks like the loft has a futon and there must be two other queen sized sleeper sofas or something similar elsewhere in the lodge.
