[[File:WaterLandscape.jpg|thumb|right|500px]]'''Mark Nilsson''' is an artist based in Hood River, OR. His work includes painting murals, art shows paintings on canvas, mosaics and illustration work. He creates unique artwork in diverse styles. His work can be "impressionistic, whimsical, abstract or the hyper realism of classic trompe l'oeil." He enjoys re-inventing himself and pursuing new mediums. ==References== [http://www.marknilsson.com/contact.asp Mark Nilsson Artist - Contact] downloaded 2015-04-21
