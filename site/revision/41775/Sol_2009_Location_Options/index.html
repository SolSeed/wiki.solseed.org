Large Group Options for Sol 2009

Oceanside is our first choice, but another coastal town would be fine too.

I think we're aiming for 30 people.

We need a large meeting space and an oceanfront view.

Some of us could camp nearby.

Options:

  • End of the Continent sleeps 22 at Gleneden Beach. My favorite in terms of how nice the house is.
  • Whale Watcher sleeps 20 in Lincoln City. Could work.
  • Captain John's Beach House sleeps 18 at Lincoln City.
  • Shear Water sleeps 16 at Depoe Bay
  • Penthouse Escape sleeps 17 in Lincoln City. "Ocean view" but 2 blocks to beach.
