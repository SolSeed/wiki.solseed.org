Opening Words

BRANDON: The SolSeed Creed is a statement of the shared values that ground the SolSeed Movement. The SolSeed Creed asserts that life and that which sustains life is good and precious, and calls humanity to mature into its role as caretaker of SolSeed. The Destiny is the ultimate expression of what nurturing and protecting life means. Becoming worthy of and fulfilling The Destiny are the two ultimate goals held by members of the SolSeed Movement.

BEN:

We believe that:

The Destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.

SHELLEY: Sol, Terra, you, me, we, ALL, are precious SolSeed.

ETHAN: As SolSeed's intelligent spark, We are its caretakers, **We must fulfill The Destiny.

BRANDON: We believe that: Our holy duty is to embrace Self Love, cultivate Empathy, and pursue Wisdom,

BEN: so that our being honors SolSeed, and our striving hastens the fulfillment of The Destiny.**

SHELLEY: Self Love is motive. Without Self Love, Empathy and Wisdom are impotent.

ETHAN: I pledge to learn to love myself; to become a person I cherish, admire, and care for.

BRANDON: Empathy is transcendent. Without Empathy, Self Love and Wisdom are evil.

BEN: I pledge to cultivate empathy in myself and others; to share and demonstrate my concern for others and for the growing life all **around me.

SHELLEY: Wisdom is effective. Without Wisdom, Self Love and Empathy are feeble and capricious.

ETHAN: I pledge to seek wisdom; to honor my past, to strive for the future, to be open to new ideas and new beliefs, and to share my wisdom **with others.

BRANDON: Through Self Love, Empathy, and Wisdom we have come to know that:

ALL: We are SolSeed; Children of the sun, Awakened by starlight, Growing, nurturing, protecting, '''We bring life!

Check In

Water Project

Water Partners International

Partner based ... nonprofit orgs on the ground to help facilitate everything they do. They don't go there very often (overhead is heavy). Working with partner orgs that know the community in need. Technology is regional dependent. Don't do same things in middle of Desert that do in Honduras.

Long term efficacy ... 98% of projects are still working 5 & 10 years later (10 years in Honduras). Not 100% still working (community interest ... not technology). Emory University and Stanford University audit. Information is on website.

Projects take 12-18 months at the shortest.

Partner with donors ... partner with orgs who then partner with the communities. Don't do for ... do with. Utilize communities resources ... only where there is interest. Must be educated in hygiene and sanitation.

  1. Hygiene Education
  2. Sanitation (latrines)
  3. Water Source

Last year administrative and fundraising costs were higher because we hired more staff and started doing more outreach and fundraising. Goal to have back down by end of next fiscal year.

Raise $10,000 for a project in an area they choose. Connect with community via pictures and correspondence. Will give us material to post on our Personal Fundraising Site and to use when we talk to people.
