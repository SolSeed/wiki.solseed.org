Here are [[Ben]]'s notes about a possible [[SolSeed]] theme song:

== Structure ==

* 4 verses, each followed by a variant of the chorus
** Verse 1: the self, [[SelfLove]], spirituality, escape from routine
** Verse 2: finding each other, [[Empathy]], building a community
** Verse 3: protecting [[life]] on [[Earth]] and helping it thrive, [[Wisdom]]
** Verse 4: [[TheDestiny]], becoming the [[seeds]] of [[Gaia]]

== Draft lyrics ==

=== Verse 1 ===

It's so hard to reach out for the stars<br>
When I live in a gray padded cell.<br>
Trapped in my head, I have drifted so far<br>
From the story my heart wants to tell.<br>
It's a story of kinship and roots, joining me to all life<br>
In a glorious family tree.<br>
It's a story of vision and wings and the wide-open sky,<br>
possibilities yearning to be.

=== Chorus ===

I'm a seed - hidden deep yet reaching for the sun
I'm a seed - invisibly my journey has begun
I'm a seed - I will find a way across the night
I'm a seed - another world is growing toward the light

==== Variants ====

* We are seeds - we will find a way...
* another world is growing into sight
* another world is ready to take flight
