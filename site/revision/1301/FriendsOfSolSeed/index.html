The [[SolSeedMovement]] has attracted people who have little use for a literal interpretation of [[TheDestiny]] or even for space travel as an inspiring story, but who feel drawn to participate by the group's strong [[values]] and friendly atmosphere. Others may find their interests align quite well, but simply need some time to learn more about [[SolSeed]] and decide whether [[JoiningTheMovement|becoming a member]] is right for them. All these people can be defined as '''Friends of SolSeed''', and belong to the much larger group of [[Kindred]] who share the basic SolSeed value of [[bringing life]].
