City Repair held its annual **[Earth Day festival](http://earthday.cityrepair.org/)** on Saturday, April 23rd, 2011, and SolSeed was there for the second year running, with three canopied booths (this time to keep out the heat, rather than the rain and hail).  The extra booth was donated to three people we met at our [first Earth Day booth](/Earth_Day_2010), Mathew and Molly representing [Cloacina](http://www.cloacina.org) and Gus representing the [Green Granges](http://www.greengranges.org/) (particularly the one in his native Silverton) and showing off his ["ecological mouse-support system"](http://highmars.org/niac/niac03a.html) experiment module.
