Opening words for the service

  • Brandon proposes working on one season at a time
  • Starting with the seventh liturgical season on the list of 8 (each one is half of a real season)

Eric's draft

The seventh liturgical season runs from the fall equinox to mid fall (aproximately November 2nd). It covers the near future, through the SolSeed movement's struggle to achieve the Destiny. What are the advances of this period? People. Practice. Dyson Trees. Space Colonies.

Barren world ... another ... barren world ...

And here ... <gasp> ... Earth!

Cities! Farms! Forests! Seas!

The SolSeed Movement forms.

In the world's nurseries, people are born.

And for every person ... there is a story!

Our story begins with the people, our members,

Those who answer the call!

Next came the practices, the movement,

Both the glue which binds us together,

And the reminders

Which align our actions

With our highest aspirations.

Then came the new trees!

The trees which diversify SolSeed,

The trees which colonize barren worlds.

Finally, SolSeed makes her colonies!

Bringing lifeless worlds to life,

freely available to all spacefaring creatures.

People. Practice. Trees. Colonies.

From barren worlds ... a family of living worlds.

Ad experiments

  • Ben focused on high-volume searches in building his table, but more specific ones are likely to get much higher click-through rates
  • Eric suggests we divide up Ben's list among us
  • Brandon prefers to start with just one, "Carl Sagan," and figure out what the results from that suggest
  • Need an ad message focused on people who searched for "Carl Sagan"
  • Hypothesis for first experiment: "There are people who resonate with our message so strongly that the presentation on our website hardly even matters, and some significant number of people who are searching for 'Carl Sagan' are among them. For our Carl Sagan advertisement, we expect a Click-Through Rate of 15% ... of those who land on our page, we expect at least 15% will click 'Our Beliefs' and that at least 2% will go all the way to submitting their email address on the Join Us page." This results in $2.50 per live email address with a population of 900 emails per month available.
