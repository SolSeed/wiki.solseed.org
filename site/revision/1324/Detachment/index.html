[[Detachment]] is an ideal that means not allowing success or failure at a project or task to control your emotions or sense of self-worth. It does not mean you don't care about the project, just that you can more easily accept failure and move on. Detachment is a major topic of the book ''[[The Untethered Soul]]''. === Related concepts from ''[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avatar_the_last_airbender Avatar: The Last Airbender]'' discussed at [[Sol 2009]] === * In the episode "The Guru," Aang is asked to open his last chakra by letting go of all worldly attachment, including his love for Katara, but refuses ** Lyric: Letting go allows you to do what you're feeling rather than what you're thinking ** Judy: Versus not caring about anything ** Lyric: Love = awareness of [[flow]]?
