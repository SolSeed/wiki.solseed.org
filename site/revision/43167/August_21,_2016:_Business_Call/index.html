Finances

  • Brandon doesn't like our accounting system very much.
  • There's no way to tease out what each of us spent and compare it to what we contributed.
  • Brandon proposes a website where we can each track what we spend with receipts.
  • Reimbursal
  • If it's more than they contributed that month, then people who spent less than they contributed would reimburse them
  • If no one spent less than they contributed…
  • Approval
  • We might need to have an organization with rules about how money can be spent.
  • Fiscal Responsibility
  • Brandon feels like we’re not very fiscally responsible and feeling the pain
  • He's almost not sure he wants to keep donating, given our outcomes for the money spent
  • He would never donate so much to a third party that is so slap-dash with its accounting and attention to outcomes
  • Ben: Goal: Predict how much things will cost and scale back plans if they're too costly?
  • Brandon: We need what we spend to create value and be intentionally aimed at that
  • Traveling to see each other is expensive in dollars but creates some amount of value

Business call scheduling

  • Ben would like to be able to leave his apartment shortly after 5:30pm every other Sunday to go to ZEGG Forum at Star House, and wants to know whether people can move the business meeting earlier in the afternoon?
  • Brandon considers whether we can move it to a different day, and do it at a time when we have energy; he doesn't have much gumption on weekday evenings
  • Ben doesn't have much in the mornings
  • Brandon proposes getting rid of Business Meetings, and only meeting on Thursdays to work on blog entries for each other's projects
  • Ben amends to having one Business Meeting per month (not on a ZEGG Forum day) so that we can track our finances
  • Brandon suggests Business Meetings could be more businessy, focused around organizational details, not strategy/vision or event planning
  • Then when we want to plan events, we'd have to have specially scheduled meetings to plan them or just use email
  • Strategy shifts could be mainly discussed at events (our unconferences have served the purpose of a strategy course correction mechanism)
  • Or we could stay more flexible
