An alternate [[SolSeed Emblem]] concept proposed by [[User:BenSibelman|Ben]]. Ideas: Starting from the image of sunrise as viewed from space, I created a symbolic version of [[Terra]] with an S marking out a yin-yang of technology (city lights) and [[Life|nature]] (clouds), trying to show them as intertwined and equal. Both will be necessary to fulfilling [[TheDestiny]], which is represented by extending the S into a "sprouting seed" with the original spaceship shape from [[Brandon]]'s emblem at the tip, a bit like a flower bud. :: ''This is way cool Ben!'' --[[Brandon]]
