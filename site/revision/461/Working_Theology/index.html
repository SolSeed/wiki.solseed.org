The SolSeed Movement is a truth, beauty and values movement. It is not a [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion religion], but it shares some of the functions of a religion by providing structure and language to support human spiritual growth. Members of the SolSeed movement embrace a diversity of faiths and belief systems that share a common theme: Life is Precious. Members of the SolSeed movement continue to embrace and deepen our commitment to various non-faith and faith traditions through our participation in SolSeed.
