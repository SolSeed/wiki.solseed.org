Buckleberry Report

Eric went up there twice last weekend

  • On Saturday I looked around the cliff facing the swamp … there's a huge amount of beaver activity there
  • There's a beaver dam holding back 5 feet of water … then the water trickles through an embankment into the lake
  • While I was looking at a stream that flows out of the lake, 6 golden eagles flew over
  • I walked up the 300-foot hill and lay down at the top, and the eagles came back
  • I also visited the nearby Lanark Eco-Village (LEV) and had interesting conversations
  • I showed Michelle the property on Sunday
