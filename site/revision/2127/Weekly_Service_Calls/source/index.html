The [[Weekly Service Calls]] are [[SolSeed Services]] for the core [[SolSeedMembership]].  They are very short, generally no more than 15 minutes, and provide a way to frequently reconnect and use spiritual practice to reaffirm our commitment to each other, to [[action]], and to [[The Destiny]].

== Logistics ==

'''When:''' Currently scheduled on Saturdays at 10:00 AM Pacific time

'''How:''' When it's just [[Brandon]], [[Shelley]], and [[Ben]], we use a two-way Skype video call.  To add more people with webcams, we've used http://tokbox.com and http://tinychat.com.  We could also connect people with audio only if desired.

'''Who:''' Each service has a leader who prepares the content and guides the service, currently rotating among [[Brandon]], [[Shelley]], and [[Ben]].  Anyone can participate, but please contact [[Shelley]] if you want to be included.

'''What:''' We ask that each participant bring a candle and matches, a glass of water, and a dish of some kind with some soil in it.

== Content ==

The services are divided into four sections.  The ''italic'' lines below are each spoken by one person, while the '''bold''' lines are spoken in unison.

=== Opening ceremony ===

''We gather to mark the passing of a week''<br>
''To align our hearts as one.''
 
''Nothing ... Still ... Nothing''

''And then ... <gasp> Something!!!!''

''First came Sol, the sun, the fire that sparks life into being.''

(Light the candles)
 
''Then came the Earth, our only home, both the shaper of life and the clay from which life makes itself.''

(Run fingers through the soil)

 
''Then came the water, the sea where the first life swam, the sea that swims inside each of us.''

(Drink the water)
 
''Finally life made the air, turning sunlight into fuel, freely available to all breathing creatures.''

(Take three deep breaths)

''Fire, Earth, Water, Air.''
