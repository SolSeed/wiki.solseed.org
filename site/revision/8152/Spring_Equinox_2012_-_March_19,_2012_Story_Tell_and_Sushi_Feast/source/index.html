=The Plan=
In Ottawa we will be telling stories and eating sushi on the 19th of March.  We do this at the spring equinox as part of an annual cycle of celebrations that celebrate the Great Story and TheDestiny.  The celebrations are based on dividing the year into four quarters which map onto the four major periods of the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_of_Evolution Great Story]: [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winter Winter]= pre-[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ediacaran Ediacaran] time, [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_(season) Spring] = the Ediacaran to the advent of human civilization, [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer Summer] = the brief candle of human civilization and the development of the ideas necessary to [[TheDestiny]] and [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autumn Fall] = the future and the reaching of [[TheDestiny]].

At the Spring Equinox we eat [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sushi sushi] especially the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nori Nori] (dried algae paper) in order to celebrate, one last time, the single celled creatures who toiled through the aeons before the Ediacarans to create the conditions necessary for complex life, especially [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Oxygenation_Event free-oxygen] and [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photosynthesis photosynthesis].  After eating we turn our attention to the multicelular life of the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phanerozoic Phanerozoic] and [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Story_telling tell stories] about life forms of the Phanerozoic.  Each member tells a story, often which they have written.  They may have written it in [http://evolutionarychristianity.com/blog/day-night-language-public-private-revelation/ realistic Day Language] or in [http://evolutionarychristianity.com/blog/day-night-language-public-private-revelation/ symbolic Night Language].  Often they have written the same story in both styles.  The idea is to build a written version of the Great Story by combining all of the stories that are told into one chronology.  Of course, it will be fiction but by crowd-sourcing a massive narrative tapestry we will begin to give an idea of the complexity and vasteness of the Great Story.

This year I know of three stories that have been written and will be told in Ottawa; we held a story writting night at [[User:EricSaumur | my]] house on Wednesday the 14th of March, 2012.  I am also told that there will be a story told as a duet accompanied by accordion!
