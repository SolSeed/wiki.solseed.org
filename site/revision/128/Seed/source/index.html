A [[Seed]] is a container for storing [[Life]] until it is ready to germinate, grow, and develop into its mature form. All [[Life]] originates from some form of [[Seed]]. Speaking not-very-scientifically, spores, bacteria, eggs, including the fertilized egg that created you, could all be considered kinds of seeds because they all contain the necessary resources and information for [[Life]] to take hold and grow.  

In purely scientific terms, seeds are small, embryonic plant life (see [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seed Wikipedia:Seed]).  

The SolSeed Movement asks us to consider the term more broadly, to contemplate [[Life]] in our solar system as a kind of [[Seed]] in storage. Each one of us constitutes an essential component of that [[Seed]], necessary for [[Life]] to grow and mature throughout the universe. 

This project of nurturing and protecting that seed, then ultimately bringing it to other worlds where it can mature is the overarching goal of our organization.  See [[TheDestiny]]
