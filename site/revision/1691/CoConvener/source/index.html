For [[OpenSpace]] events, [[CoConvener]]s agree to do the following:
: 1) attend and bring energy to the event
: 2) send invitations to folks you'd like to hang out with
: 3) chip in on the logistics

In addition to these essential agreements, [[CoConvener]]s often help shape the initial invitation.
