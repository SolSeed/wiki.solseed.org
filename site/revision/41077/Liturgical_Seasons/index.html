The eight liturgical seasons of the SolSeed Movement, each with its own ritual used during the [[Weekly Service Calls]], represent a special kind of [[SolSeed Cosmic Calendar | Cosmic Calendar]]. Each season culminates in a [[Celebration]]. * 1 - [[Genesis]] Winter Solstice to Mid Winter * 2 - [[Conflatorium]] Mid Winter to Spring Equinox * 3 - [[Viriditas de Terra]] Spring Equinox to Mid Spring * 4 - [[Diversitas]] Mid Spring to Summer Solstice * 5 - [[Sophos]] Summer Solstice to Mid Summer * 6 - [[Civis]] Mid Summer to Fall Equinox * 7 - [[Viventibus Mundos]] Fall Equinox to Mid Fall * 8 - [[Viventibus Galaxia]] Mid Fall to Winter Solstice
