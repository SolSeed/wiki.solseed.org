One-Page Plan threads

Why: Life is Precious

What: Expressing the Arete of Life (The Destiny is a big piece of that)

  • Vibrantly alive individuals, groups, communities, ecosystems and worlds
  • Diverse, self-expressed, empowered, free beings
  • Caring relationships that transcend our individual selves
  • Community that calls forth the best in us
  • Healthy sustainable ecosystems
  • supporting environmental restoration
  • releasing improved life forms on Earth (super trees; ocean trees)
  • Life radiating out from Earth to colonize the trillions of empty worlds that await
  • using Dyson trees to send life through our galaxy

How: Motivate, Instruct, and Support

  1. Inspiration and motivation for the elephant
  • Exploring wonderfully cool things
  • Basking in artful expressions of wonder (Arete of life, cosmic religious feeling, etc)
  • Creating wonder evoking artifacts
  • Promoting books and videos that evoke wonder
  • Engaging in individual and collective rituals that infuse our lives with meaning
  1. Instructions for the rider (Detailed, Practical Educational Resources)
  • Expositions from our world view
  • science
  • philosophy
  • religion (both SolSeed and general ideas about advanced religion/Kindred)
  • community
  • Recipes
  • How to perform various personal growth exercises (meditation etc)
  • How to do some scientific experiments yourself
  • How to host a particular kind of event
  • How to form a new seed community
  • How to make cool things
  1. Systems and structures that support the rider/elephant

a. SolSeed Communities/Villages

  • Recruiting members
  • Living in close proximity
  • Building eco-friendly structures to live, work, and hold events in

    • b. Self-aware and Vibrant Kindred Movement

  • Holding festivals
  • Granting fellowships
  • Networking with kindred groups

    • c. The Religion Developed

  • Establishing Practices
  • Developing our canon

    • d. Founding businesses and non-profits

  • Creating organizations who make cool things.

Note: Cool things are things that strive toward theDestiny
