To raise awareness of the [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) and get people interested in joining or collaborating with us, we are working on various forms of media describing who we are and what we're about.

- [This website](/SolSeed)
  - We plan to have a static welcome page
  - We may move the wiki to a site with a more user-friendly editing system
- [SolSeed books](/SolSeed_books)
  - *[Happy in the Sun](/Happy_in_the_Sun)* (children's book)
  - *[ The Book of Life](/SolSeed:_The_Book_of_Life)* (a SolSeed "bible," including prose, poems, songs, etc, useful for [SolSeed Services](/SolSeed_Services))
  - *[ Helping Life Thrive, On Earth And Beyond](/SolSeed:_Helping_Life_Thrive,_On_Earth_And_Beyond)* (book within the Book of Life that will also be published separately)
- [SolSeed videos](/Videos)
  - [Pick a Future](/Pick_a_Future) (6-minute music video, unpublishable due to music copyright)
  - [Reach for the Stars](/Reach_for_the_Stars) (planned music video based on [The Seed](/Song:_The_Seed))
  - [Gaia's Heartbeat](/Gaia's_Heartbeat) (music video with time-lapse videos of [Gaia](/Gaia) to help viewers connect emotionally with the idea of Earth as a living organism)
  - [Boxy and Tinman on Why SolSeed is a Religion](/Boxy_and_Tinman_on_Why_SolSeed_is_a_Religion)
  - [30-second spot](/30-second_spot)
  - [Longer video](/Longer_video) (currently untitled, but planned to be about 15 minutes long)
  - [Something You Learn](/Something_You_Learn) (music video based on a song by Lori Henriques)
  - [Rise Anew](/Rise_Anew) (music video based on the concept of [recovering from ecological catastrophe](/Bringing_life_to_our_world#Crossing_the_gap))
- [Newsletters](/Newsletters) to update our [members](/members) and [friends](/FriendsOfSolSeed) on our projects and plans.
- [Facebook group](http://www.facebook.com/group.php?gid=232314258760)
- Music
  - [SolSeedSong](/SolSeedSong)

There are also some other folks producing media we'd like to promote:

- There's a band in Eugene, Oregon (formerly Medford) that independently came up with the name [Sol Seed](https://www.youtube.com/user/SolSeedMusic).  Their music is great, and they have some surprisingly evocative lyrics like "...to create a destiny psycho-synergistically with Gaia and the galaxy..."
- [Shawn Kilburn](/Shawn's_space_story) of Seattle is working on a wonderfully whimsical space adventure story.
