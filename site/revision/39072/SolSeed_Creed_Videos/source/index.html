=== Opportunity Sketch ===

Get passers by to accept being videoed for U-tube performing the SolSeed Creed.

=== Outcomes ===

Increased awareness of SolSeed through talking to members of the public (people on the street) about SolSeed.
Videos are available for use in promoting SolSeed.

=== Impact ===

Increased awareness of SolSeed.
Increased impact of other promotions through use of videos

=== Cost ===

$1000

20 hrs

=== Steps ===
* Make SolSeed T-shirts
* Make videos of ourselves wearing the T-shirts and saying the creed with feeling
* Set up outdoor studio in a carefully chosen location with
 ** lots of pedestrian traffic
 ** very nearby, out-of-the-path, natural and beautiful backdrop
* Outdoor studio should include a privacy booth for changing into the T-shirts
* A sign-up table with video release forms and an explanatory backdrop
* Multiple cameras on tripods and a good outdoor microphone
* A computer that is hooked to all cameras and the microphone so that each subject's video can be spliced together into a single video while they wait.
* After each day of shooting, post the videos to a SolSeed U-tube channel.
