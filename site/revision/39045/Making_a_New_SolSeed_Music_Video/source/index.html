=== Opportunity Sketch ===

Get a new song that evokes the values and vision of SolSeed, and use both new and existing imagery to illustrate it in video form.

=== Outcome ===

Release a SolSeed music video to YouTube, on iTunes (?), and on our website.

=== Impact ===

Attract potential members and allies with an inspiring short video that embodies much of what we value.

=== Cost ===

Minimal dollar cost

500 hrs

=== Steps ===
* Finish writing the [[SolSeedThemeSong]] lyrics
* Work with John and Will to complete the song we earned by supporting the Terra Lumina Kickstarter
* Revise the [[Pick a Future]] music video script to match the new song
* Obtain new visuals from public domain and Creative Commons sources
* Possibly purchase rights to some royalty-free imagery
* Create new graphics as needed
* Animate the imagery in time to the music
* Revise the resulting video
* Release to YouTube, iTunes (?), our website and other places
* Promote the release
