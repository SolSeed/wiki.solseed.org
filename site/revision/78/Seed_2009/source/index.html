== Invitation ==
You are receiving this invitation because someone wants to see you at [[Seed2009]].  [[Seed2009]] is a retreat and [[OpenSpace]] event hosted by members and friends of the [[SolSeedMovement]].  The theme question for the event is: '''What does it mean to "Bring Life" to each of us, to all of us, and to the entire galaxy?'''

To learn more, visit [[Seed2009]].  If you are planning to attend add yourself to [[Seed_2009/Participants|the participants list]].

== Where ==
[[FalconsCrestLodge]] on Mount Hood, about 1 hour from Portland sleeps 24 and has numerous cozy spaces for [[OpenSpace]] sessions and a satellite Internet connection.

== When ==
January 9,10, and a half day on January 11.  We have [[FalconsCrestLodge|the lodge]] from 3pm Thursday January 8 through 11am Monday morning January 12.  For more details see [[Seed2009/Schedule|the schedule]].
