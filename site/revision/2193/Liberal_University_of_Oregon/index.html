This was a session at [[Seed 2011]]. == Participants == * [[Gus]] (convener) * [[Ben]] (note-taker) * [[Lyric]] * [[Brandon]] * [[Shelley]] * [[Sequoia]] * [[Molly]] * [[Mathew]] == Notes == * http://liberaluniversity.org ** First non-religiously-affiliated university in Oregon *** Known as "the atheist college" ** Lasted from 1892-1902 (formally founded in 1897) *** Occupied the only "Liberal Hall" in Oregon that wasn't out in the country ** Gus is assembling archival material on the website, as well as new stuff * Historical context ** Silver Falls Timber Company was Silverton's big industry until they chopped all the trees down *** [http://liberaluniversity.org/luosftcpan.php Quicktime VR] of the timber operation from a panoramic photo taken using a camera similar to the ones on the Viking landers ** Germ theory was just discovered ** "Free Love" movement at the time, offshoot of Free Thought, was opposed to marriage because it constituted enslavement of women ** Silverton Oak was chopped down around that time by what Homer Davenport called "the new element, men with higher collars and smaller hats" ** First air mail to fly out of Silverton was flown by a female pilot * People involved in LUO ** James K. Buff *** Protested fashion by wearing outrageous costumes *** Tended to walk long distances ** Homer C. Davenport *** Was considered a lazy kid in Silverton *** Hired by Hearst in San Francisco, became highest-paid political cartoonist of the era in New York *** Gus did an annotated edition of the book of his cartoons 5 years ago *** Father Timothy was a prominent Republican who prevented Oregon from becoming a slave state and decried mistreatment of Native Americans ** Pearl W. Geer, co-founder *** Related to first native-born governor of Oregon *** Later went into vaudeville and did sound effects *** Family now runs a CSA called Geercrest Farm ** Prof. J.E. Hosmer *** Zealous temperance advocate *** Quit LUO because they didn't fit his model of Free Thought, claiming he would move to California, then bought the local newspaper instead and used it for anti-LUO campaign *** Also attacked Catholic Church, which sued him for libel and won ** Jake McClaine *** Banker who was a major community benefactor **** Rode at the head of funeral procession **** Funded the trombone band *** Supposedly the only Democrat in Silverton at the time ** MacGert Cooley *** Violin and cornet instructor at LUO *** Also in the Silverton Marine Band *** Kicked in the head by a horse as a child * [http://liberaluniversity.org/luo02.php Courses] ** Grange has been wanting to put together courses on sustainability, permaculture, etc ** 4 areas: *** Physical sciences and math *** Biologics, including agriculture *** Social sciences *** Environment, art, and health ** Will have some "Extraterrestrial Agriculture" courses ** Uses open-source Learning Management System called [http://moodle.org Moodle]
