It's time for explicit organizations to begin coalescing within the SolSeedMovement.

Purposes of a SolSeedMovement non-profit

  • Events ... plan, receive donations, and pay for events like Seed2009, Sol2009, and Kindred2010
  • GrantMaking ... collect donations for and disburse to other life-bringing organizations we wish to support
  • clean drinking water
  • supporting mothers
  • educating girls
  • space research
  • Projects ... recognize opportunities in our own communities that aren't being served by anyone and organize them
  • neighborhood seed bank
  • space scouts (special chapters of boy/girl scouts)
