It's time for explicit organizations to begin coalescing within the [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement).

Purposes of a [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) non-profit
- [Events](/Events) ... plan, receive donations, and pay for events like [Seed2009](/Seed2009), [Sol2009](/Sol2009), and [Kindred2010](/Kindred2010)
- [GrantMaking](/GrantMaking) ... collect donations for and disburse to other life-bringing organizations we wish to support
  - clean drinking water
  - supporting mothers
  - educating girls
  - space research
- [Projects](/Projects) ... recognize opportunities in our own communities that aren't being served by anyone and organize them
  - neighborhood seed bank
  - space scouts (special chapters of boy/girl scouts)
