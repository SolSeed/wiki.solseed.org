The Exaltation is the last section of the [[SolSeed Creed]].  It may be used instead of the creed when time or attention spans are short.

=== Exaltation ===

:[[Sign for Through|Through]] [[Sign for Passion|Passion]], [[Sign for Empathy|Empathy]], and [[Sign for Wisdom|Wisdom]]
:we have come to know that:

::We are SolSeed
::children of the [[Sign for Earth|Earth]] and [[Sign for Sun|Sun]]
::awakened by starlight
::growing
::nurturing
::discovering
::We Bring Life!
