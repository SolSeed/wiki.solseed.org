[[SolSeed]] is not a religion, but it has some things in common with the world's religions: it provides some ideas of how people can live together in harmony (through the [[SolSeedCreed]] and [[SolSeedAnthropology]]), while looking forward to the almost spiritual [[TheDestiny|Destiny]] of acting as seeds to spread [[Life]] to other worlds. The ideals and goals of [[SolSeed]] ought to be integratable without conflict into any kind of faith.
