The [[SolSeedServices]] are regular in-person gatherings presided over by [[Shelley Sanders]], at [[Brandon]] and Shelley's home in Portland, Oregon.

They are designed to be spiritual but not [[SolSeed and Religion|religious]], creating a space for the appreciation of [[life]], of each other, and of the sacred.  The goal is to create and nurture a sort of "spiritual [[SolSeedCommunity|community]]."

Each service has a "seed" topic that guides discussion as well as choices of music, poetry, and other [[Creativity|creative]] aspects of the service.
