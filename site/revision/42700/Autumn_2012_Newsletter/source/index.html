**This season, we focused in on our priority projects and took a few big steps.**

![400px](/assets/uploads/Rocket_launch.JPG)

We all had a great time launching rockets to represent [The Destiny](/The_Destiny), as part of the Portland and [Ottawa](http://www.meetup.com/SolSeed-Ottawa/events/91063842/) Solstice celebrations on December 21st and 22nd.  The map of the United States on the asphalt was a nice touch, allowing the Portland contingent to imagine the city as the site of a major spaceport.  Each celebration also featured a potluck dinner with family and friends.

![200px](/assets/uploads/BookInHand-Open.jpg)

We moved toward publishing the big [SolSeed book](/SolSeed_book) (title under review) by translating the text into LaTeX format, which allows us to define margins, title pages, and so on.  We even had a partial test copy printed to find out what it would look like.

![200px](/assets/uploads/Terra_Lumina_album_cover.jpg)

The CD of songs about science that we helped to produce, [Terra Lumina](http://terralumina.bandcamp.com/album/terra-lumina), was released on December 18th! John and Will should now have some time to work on the SolSeed song, using [lyrics](/SolSeedSong) that I completed in November.

This winter, we will: 
- continue work on the book 
- have a retreat to revisit and refine our overall purpose 
- go skating in February to celebrate Snowball Earth 
- hopefully get the finished song from John and Will and start working on a new music video! 

Thanks for reading!  

Ben Sibelman
