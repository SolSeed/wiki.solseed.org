### Waverly Commons
- In inner SE Portland but farther south & east than the current Sanders house
- Are they knocking down existing buildings to build these "brand new custom homes"?
  - Google Maps imagery suggests that the existing Trillium Family Services building was knocked down
- The community shares our environmental values with green building, gardening, and sharing tools, and of course kid-friendliness
- Brandon imagines building a house with a 2-story basement that takes up almost the whole footprint of the lot
  - Bottom story would be a SolSeed sanctuary that is large, very high-ceilinged, beautiful and attractive as a place to visit, with alcoves helping to illustrate deep time
    - Ben suggests a spiral staircase in the middle
  - Upper basement would be a workshop (wood and metalworking equipment, 3D printers and other electronics, etc)
  - Ground floor (normal house size) would have a fellowship hall as a hub for the Waverly Commons community
  - Top floor(s) would be where the Sanders live
  - Likely cost: around $800,000
- The community already has 5 houses in various stages of construction; there may be 2 or 3 lots left now, but some people will probably drop out of the process
- The Sanders found out about this last night, but have pretty much already decided to do it (although the scale may become more modest)
