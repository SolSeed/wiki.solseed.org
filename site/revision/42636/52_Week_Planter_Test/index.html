Opportunity Profile

Opportunity Sketch

Plan and execute a 13 week rice planting/harvesting test in order to help us design the 52 Week Planter. The test will include a wide variety of containers, types of rice, and procedures for cultivating the rice.

Outcome

A decent understanding of the planting/harvesting cycle for rice, including the conditions that help it to thrive.

Impact

One step closer to a daily/weekly practice that connects us with the cycle of life and reminds us of the importance of steady, systematic cultivation of our most important works.

Cost

$200

20 hrs up front

1 minute per day every day for 13 weeks
