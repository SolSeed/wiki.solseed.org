=== Our blogs ===
We're going to start posting articles to http://blog.solseed.org/ and other aggregators like http://humanisticpaganism.com/

=== Getting feedback on posts ===
If you'd like feedback on a potential blog post do the following:
* create a link on this page
* follow the redlink to create the page
* edit the post as much as you like
* when you are ready to share it, copy and paste the actual text of the post into an email to those you'd like feedback from
* <b>put a full link to the wiki version at the top of the email</b>

=== Posts seeking feedback ===

Please leave any comments for the post's author on the discussion page for the post (not here).  Also, feel warmly welcomed to suggest changes by actually editing the post.
