[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeedMovement]] From the [[SolSeedCreed]] ---- [[Image:SolSeedEmblemThumbnail.jpg|right]] : We believe that: :: [[TheDestiny]] of [[SolSeed]] :: is to take root amongst the stars. :: [[Sol]], ::: [[Terra]], :::: you, ::::: me, :::::: we, ::::::: ALL, :: are precious [[SolSeed]]. :: As [[SolSeed]]'s intelligent spark, :: WE are its caretakers, :: WE must fulfill [[TheDestiny]]. Members of the [[SolSeedMovement]] are united by a single tenet: Life is Precious. We invite you to '''[[JoiningTheMovement|join our community]]'''. The SolSeedMovement exists to support '''all''' people who believe that life is precious. The [[SolSeedMovement]] is a practical philosophy, a praxis, a way of being in community with the body of all life. We are a coalition of diverse people brought together by the core values that we all share. Rather than spend our energy on questions of faith, politics, or policy that too often divide us, we are working out what it means to live our shared truth, beauty, and values. Working alone, we can each have an impact on our local community, but working together we can change the world! The [[SolSeed]] Movement is a "Truth, Beauty, and Values Movement": : The [[SolSeed]] movement is a [[TruthMovement]] because it pursues truth (i.e., the concepts that best model observable reality) via the scientific method. (See [[Wisdom]].) : The [[SolSeed]] movement is a [[BeautyMovement]] because it embraces the power of human passion and seeks to encourage it through the development of the arts. (See [[SelfLove]].) : The [[SolSeed]] movement is a [[ValuesMovement]] because it cultivates moral behavior that is grounded on the assertion that all life is precious and good. (See [[Empathy]].) Please feel warmly invited to join us in shaping the body of all life into a community of sharing that cares for each of us.
