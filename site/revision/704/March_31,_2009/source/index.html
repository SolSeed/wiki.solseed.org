The Destiny: A Reader's Theatre

<i>Brenna:</i> The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
<br><i>Stacey:</i> I don't get that. What does that mean?
<br><i>Brenna:</i> Well, destiny is kind of what's going to happen . . . and what we <i>want</i> to happen. 

<i>Brandon:</i> The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
<br><i>Ben:</i> So, that means spaceships, right?
<br><i>Brandon:</i> Well, it does for some. 

<i>Shelley:</i> The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
<br><i>Mark:</i> That sounds like it's important to you. Tell me more.
<br><i>Shelley:</i> Well, I do like the idea of embracing our shared present and future together -- our destiny.

<i>Boni:</i> The destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
<br><i>Bob:</i> Say more.
<br><i>Boni:</i> I need to think about it a little more. The destiny means different things for every person. 

<i>Mark:</i> So, what you guys are saying is . . .
<br><i>Stacey:</i> They're saying that the destiny of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars.
<br><i>Mark:</i> Maybe we need to talk a little more.
