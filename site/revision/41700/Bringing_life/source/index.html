### Definition
Bringing life is contributing to the Upward Spiral of life.  

### Scope
It is both a global endeavour and a personal or individual endeavour.  It is carrying with you the intent to increase the diversity and intensity of life.  It is literally carrying life into barren places and helping it to establish itself.

### Individual Action
As an individul, you can bring life by contributing to [SolSeed](/SolSeed):
- Bring food to a potluck
- Practice medicine or first aid
- Practice art or craft - create beauty
- Garden
- Provide support to anyone doing any of the above.

### Global Action
As a civilization, we can bring life by:
- carrying life to other worlds and establishing it there,
- caring for the life on Earth,
- creating life forms that can live in the less intense parts of the Earth and nurturing them into forests in those places.

### Bringing
To bring is to move and to carry something with you.  Bringing life is a spiritual concept so the movement can be physical locomotion or can be spiritual exploration.  

### Life
On a global scale it is all forms of life from all of the six [kingdoms](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_(biology)) of life; "Eubacteria, Archaebacteria, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia."  On an individual scale it [power](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_(physics)) being used create beauty.
