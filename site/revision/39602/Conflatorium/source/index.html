The season of [[Conflatorium]] (melting; tentative name) is the second liturgical season in the SolSeed Movement's Liturgical Calendar.  It symbolises the emergence of complex life from Iceball Earth 590 million years ago.  During this season the [[Weekly Service Calls]] may use the following draft Opening ceremony:

''We gather to mark the passing of a week''<br>
''To align our hearts as one.''

(Light the Sol candle)
(take an ice cube from bowl and hold it in your hand)

''Ice ... just ... ice ...''<br>
''and then ... [gasp] ... Melting''

(Drop the ice cube into warm/hot water)

''Warm Sun! Open Water! Dry Land! Scented Breeze!''
''a sea teaming with life ... ''<br>
''Under the frozen ice, new lifeforms were born.''<br>
''And now they emerge to tell their story!''

(pour the ice cubes (or big block of ice) that have things (e.g., trilobites) frozen in them, into the bowl of warm water)

''This story begins with cells, joining to sister cells,''<br>
''the invention that grew the individual to any size!''

(Participants clasp hands, or join two hands clasped if you are alone)

''Next came the mouth, the first structure,''<br>
''Both the feeder of animals,''<br>
''and the singularity (imperative/discontinuity/focus?) that ''<br>
''gave animals direction.''

("Chase" and then eat some food, e.g. seaweed, suishi, shrimp, or goldfish crackers ... participant's choice)

''Then came the senses!''<br>
''The sensing which gave the first animals reason to swim.''<br>
''The sensing which still directs us today!''

(Put fingers on mouth, nose, eye, temple, and ear, thus touching all five senses)

''Finally, Animals secreted calcium,''<br>
''Armour, Shells, Teeth, Bones, ''<br>

(Shake the fossil shakers)

''Massive, piling, surfaces folded through open water,''<br>
''A billion allies emerged,''<br>
''Life learned to make new reefs, new worlds,''

(building motions of one hand on top of another going up up up?)

''Cells Joined to Cells. "<br>
"Mouths. Senses. Shells.''

''From silent ice ... teeming reefs!''

(some motion for the kids)
