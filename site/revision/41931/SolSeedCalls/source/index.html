Because the [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) has [members](/JoiningTheMovement) who live in widely separated locations, many of its "meetings" are by phone.  Notes taken during some of these calls can be found at [Group Experiences#SolSeedCalls](/Group_Experiences#SolSeedCalls).  Here is a description of the different types of call and how you can get involved:

### Online Work Bees
**What:** Discussing and working on the current major projects of the [SolSeedMovement](/SolSeedMovement) (ex. event planning, book writing, choosing sites and architects for the [SolSeedVillage](/SolSeedVillage)).

**When:** Thursdays at 6:30-7:30 PM Pacific, and Sundays at 5:30-6:30 PM Pacific, with occasional variations.

**Who:** Currently just [Brandon](/Brandon), [Ben](/Ben), [Eric](/Eric), and [Shawn](/Shawn).

**How:** We've been using Skype group video calls.  We can also connect people with audio only if desired.  Contact [Brandon](/Brandon) if you'd like to be included.  Also, we use [EtherPad](http://thesanders.us/SolSeed) to take notes and post them to [Group Experiences#SolSeedCalls](/Group_Experiences#SolSeedCalls).

### [Weekly Service Calls](/Weekly_Service_Calls)
**What:** These are [SolSeed Services](/SolSeed_Services) for the core [SolSeedMembership](/SolSeedMembership).  They are very short, generally no more than 15 minutes, and provide a way to frequently reconnect and use spiritual practice to reaffirm our commitment to each other, to [action](/action), and to [The Destiny](/The_Destiny).

**When:** Saturdays at 9:00 AM Pacific time, with occasional variations.

**Who:** Each service has a leader who prepares the content and guides the service, currently rotating among [Brandon](/Brandon), [Shelley](/Shelley),[Ben](/Ben), and [Eric](/Eric).  Anyone can participate, but please contact [Shelley](/Shelley) if you want to be included.

**How:** We've been using Skype group video calls.  We could also connect people with audio only if desired. We ask that each participant bring a candle and matches, as well as various other supplies depending on the [time of year](/Liturgical_Seasons).

### [Peer Coaching Triads](/Peer_Coaching_Triads) (dormant)
**What:** A sort of mini-support group system invented by [Ted](/Ted), where participants take turns being the Coach, Client, and Observer.  Each person will play each role once during a roughly one-hour-long call.
- The Coach's job is to make the 7-to-12-minute session "all about the client" and try to ask questions that help the Client to help him/herself progress on whatever issue they wanted to talk about.  (Sometimes the topic is a positive event in the Client's life, in which case the Coach's role becomes less clear.)
- The Client's job is to be creative, resourceful, and whole, and to pick a topic that is the most important thing for him/her to talk about at the time of the call.
- The Observer's job is to silently listen to the conversation and then give his/her opinions afterward about how well the Coach did, along with any suggestions for improvement.

**When:** Varies depending on the triad.
