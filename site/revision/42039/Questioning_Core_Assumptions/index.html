Discussion

  • Current scientific methods are about separation (isolating phenomena, keeping the observer from affecting the experiment, etc)
  • There is a paradigm shift afoot to more systems-focused science, where we look at relationships and flows
  • Aspects of the new system
  • Acknowledges that people are not rational
  • Helps avoid long periods of denial before accepting a new model that better fits the evidence
  • Which in turn helps science adapt to the rapid pace of change in the modern world
  • Example: new version of the ether theory, positing a plasma (instead of a solid) that establishes an absolute reference frame
  • Acknowledges that many steps in science are subjective, not objective
  • Sees objects as momentary accumulations in a flow
  • Transcends and includes description of the world as objects
