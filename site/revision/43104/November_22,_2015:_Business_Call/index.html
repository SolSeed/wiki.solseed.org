Strategic Planning

Spend 5 minutes writing and 5 minutes sharing the ideas written. What is our vision for what we are going to do for several hours a week for the next year, rather than the outcome.

Ben

During the next year I want to spend several hours every week creating media for SolSeed, to help us connect with people on a visceral level to share our beliefs and positive vision of the future. Some of this may be tailored to specific target audiences. This can look like:

  • writing book chapters on the wiki
  • writing blog entries and posting them to our blog and sometimes Humanistic Paganism
  • creating graphics in Photoshop or Illustrator and uploading or printing them
  • putting together simple videos in Movie Maker, Final Cut Pro, and/or Motion and uploading them to YouTube

    • At the moment I have more ideas for blog entries than for any other type of media, but will be working on the Gaia Kickstarter video a lot in the near future.

Eric

  • During the next year I want to spend several hours every week building a robot. The end goal is the spider robot powered by hydraulics or pneumatics and designed to climb trees but the intermediate robots will have fewer legs and less versatility; they will only climb on specific surfaces or they will walk but not climb etc…
  • During the next year I want to spend several hours every week meditating with at least three partners (Ben, Brandon and [To be Recruited]) in order to spread the benefits of partner meditation to more people. Of course, as with Ben and Brandon, [Toby Recruited] will miss days due to other commitments but the intent will be to meditate most days.
  • During the next year I want to build the Gaia’s People Group into a functioning group dedicated to the body of all Life. Building this group will involve scheduling fun and on-theme meetups for the group on at least a monthly basis.

Brandon

During the next year I want to spend several hours every week working toward friendly AI. Working toward friendly AI follows an arc of development that begins with a literature survey phase. Doing the literature survey consists of looping over papers:

  • Identify a core paper that I want to dissect
  • As I read the paper, tear it apart and put the pieces into a public facing wiki
  • As I collect the pieces from these various papers I will build a world of AI concepts with various themed tours through that world.
  • Select the next paper.

I’m not sure what phase two looks like, but I think it involves some coding and continued surveying.
