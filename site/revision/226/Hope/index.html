[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeedPhilosophy]] < [[Hope]] <i> : [[Hope]] is the motivation to fight for a better future. : Without [[Hope]], :: [[Pragmatism]] would teach us that [[Creativity]] and [[Perseverance]] are pointless, :: since few will fight for a cause they believe to be lost. </i> : "Hope. It is the quintessential human delusion, simultaneously the source of your greatest strength and your greatest weakness."<br> -the Architect of the Matrix, in [http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0234215/ The Matrix Reloaded] (just about the only philosophically interesting line of dialog in the movie) In other words: when the situation is grim and the odds are against us, hope keeps us fighting because we believe, however foolishly, that we are going to make it through. Sometimes, hope means accepting that things may well get worse before they get better, and indeed that they may get better precisely because catastrophe will force us learn from our terrible mistakes (see [[Cautious Optimism]]).
