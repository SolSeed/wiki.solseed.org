== Kindred 2010 ==
* Brandon and Shelley have looked at the Windward campground location
** They have 30 years of experiments with living on the land, reuse of materials, etc
** Campground is not an ideal location
*** Unpaved road
*** Port-a-potties
*** No particularly scenic natural setting
** Fee is $4/person/day, but not a whole lot cheaper overall than other campgrounds
* We could have a 1-day Open Space in Portland as a run-up to a Longest Day Party
** Or just make it a really great outdoor party, while still inviting kindred groups (space enthusiasts, intentional communities, environmentalists)
* Meanwhile, get more co-conveners to take on logistics, maybe for a September timeframe for Kindred 2010
** That way we don't have to decide on a location right now
