''[[Happy in the Sun]]'' is a children's book that tells the story of a "curious little rocket" learning about the relationship between [[being]] and [[action|doing]].  We plan to self-publish it in early 2010.

''[[SolSeed: The Book of Life]]'' will be a compilation of many texts including prose, poetry, and songs. It will help provide material for [[SolSeed Services]], among other things.

''[[SolSeed: Helping Life Thrive, On Earth And Beyond]]'' will be a book within the Book of Life but also published separately. It will describe our [[SolSeedPhilosophy|philosophy]], the [[SolSeedMovement]]'s plans, and our broader ideas about what a [[starfarers|starfaring]] civilization would look like and how we might build one.  It should be useful in finding potential new [[members]], but writing it will also help us crystallize our own thinking about what we're building.  Our current [[OnePagePlan]] goal is to publish it by the end of 2010.
