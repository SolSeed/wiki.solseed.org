##### What are the threads we would have illustrated?
Why: **Life is Precious**

What: **Expressing the Arete of Life** (The Destiny is a big piece of that)
- Founding businesses and non-profits
- Advancing human space travel and colonization
- Sending life in the form of Dyson trees
- Supporting environmental restoration on Earth

How: **Motivate, Instruct, and Support**

1. Inspiration and motivation for the elephant 
- Creating artful expressions of wonder (Arete of life, cosmic religious feeling, etc)
  - Publishing books that evoke wonder
  - Publishing videos
- Integrating our media into educational curricula
- Making cool things

2. Instructions for the rider
- Detailed, Practical Educational Resources
- Developing educational curricula, especially for science
- Publishing textbooks (that include some expression of wonder but are mostly analytical)

3. Systems and structures that support the rider/elephant

a. SolSeed Communities/Villages
- Recruiting members
- Living in close proximity
- Building eco-friendly structures to live, work, and hold events in
b. Self-aware and Vibrant Kindred Movement
- Holding festivals
- Granting fellowships
- Networking with kindred groups
c. The Religion Developed
- Establishing [Practices](/Practices)
- Developing our canon

**SolSeed has Trees** (What)

**SolSeed has Extreme Trees** (What)
- We have bred trees that can live in extreme climates and are experimenting with growing them in those climates

**SolSeed has a Dyson Tree Lab** (What)
- We have a greenhouse capable of controlling pressure and temperature down to vacuum and 30 K and are using it to breed trees gradually toward being able to survive those conditions.

**SolSeed has Dyson Trees** (What)
