<small>Note: There is [[SolSeedCreed|another version]] of the creed which is not formatted for group reading.</small> Also: [http://solseed.org/we_believe We Believe], a book of commentary and illustrations unpacking each verse of the creed. The SolSeed Creed is a statement of the shared values that ground the SolSeed Movement. The SolSeed Creed asserts that life and that which sustains life is good and precious, and calls humanity to mature into its role as caretaker of SolSeed. The Destiny is the ultimate expression of what nurturing and protecting life means. Becoming worthy of and fulfilling The Destiny are the two ultimate goals held by members of the SolSeed Movement.
