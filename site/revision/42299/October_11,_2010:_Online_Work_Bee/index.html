Seeds of Gaia: Going to space for the sake of Life

Join us to discuss the flowering of Gaia into places beyond Earth. In this future, spaceships are little worlds unto themselves, filled with living ecosystems that sustain spacefarers' souls even as they maintain the air, water, and food supply. The mission of these "seed ships" has more to do with bringing life to other worlds than taking resources out of them to feed human industry. What are the historical roots of this idea? Which groups most closely hold this vision today? What are they up to? How can you get involved?
