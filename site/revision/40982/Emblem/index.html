[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeed Emblem]] There is no official Emblem for SolSeed, but several have seen fairly wide use. With the most prevalent being some form the the sunrise rocket flower bud (it needs a better name) shown below. Read on for more of the history of where this came from. == Current == This is an "earth as seed" emblem, with an explicit yin and yang, plus an obvious womb shape. Note also the three leaves ... for [[Passion]], [[Wisdom]], and [[Empathy]] and the rocket shaped flower. It also evokes the upward spiral. The yin-yang shape is also a giant S for SolSeed. The one above treats the Earth as a sprouting seed. This one reverses the metaphor and looks at spaceships, going out to plant and grow new living worlds, as the seeds.
