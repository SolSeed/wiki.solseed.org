[[Category:Topical Community Protocol]] === [[EricSaumur]]'s [[Topical Community Protocol | Protocol]] === ====Meetup.com/solseed-ottawa==== * I will maintain three series of events on this site ** The season marking series: 8 Events per year 2 Solstices, 2 Equinoxes and 4 mid season events ** Seeing Nature Book Club: Monthly with a break for some summer months ** Seeing Nature Excursions: Weekly summer only ====Facebook==== * I will make several distinct efforts on Facebook ** Friendings : I will make an average of one friend request per day until I reach 1000 friends ** SolSeed posts : I will share one SolSeed post per week ** Friend content likes/comments/shares : I will like, comment on or share at least one thing that a friend has put up per week. There is a tendency to tit-for-tat in terms of likes: Like your friends stuff and they will like yours. ** Invite friends : I will invite two friends to like SolSeed each week. ==== Miscelaneous social activies ==== * Attending social activities creates oportunities to meet new people. Eventually, I can explain SolSeed to them. Also I can friend them on Facebook (see above). ** Other Meetups and Unitarian Universalist Services (I currently include the following meetups in this category: CFI, Less Wrong, Skeptics or Ottawa Activities, plus I also include Unitarian Universalist Services, ON/NO events and Overbrook Community Association meetings and events in this category. ** I will attend two events from this category every month. ** Other Social Activities : I will accept opportunities to attend social activities as they become available (accounting for conflicts of course) * Other opportunities : I will break my protocol as necessary in order to take other actions that I perceive as more productive. SolSeed is an experiment; it is new; it is better to try new methods as often as possible than to stick to the tried and true because we are too young to have a tried and true method. === Protocol Changes === I will change my protocol without notice if any of the following occur: * I see a new oportunity that looks better than what I am doing. * I find the pace is making me sick, exhausted or otherwise is not bringing life to me * I find the pace is interfering with my family or work life and is not bringing life to them. __NOTOC__
