We are inspired by [[Earthseed]] but have grown in our own direction.  While the ideas of observing and shaping change are fundamental to who we are we haven't focused on "God is [[Change]]".  This stems from our observation that there is no real value system in Earthseed other than being in touch with what is and being the active architects of our lives.

We've evolved to think of God more as the [[upward spiral]] of [[life]].    For example, consider this Earthseed verse and try substituting either the word Change or the word Life for God.
