An alternate [SolSeed Emblem](/SolSeed_Emblem) concept proposed by [Ben](/User:BenSibelman).

Ideas: Starting from the image of sunrise as viewed from space, I created a symbolic version of [Terra](/Terra) with an S marking out a yin-yang of technology (city lights) and [nature](/Life) (clouds), trying to show them as intertwined and equal.  Both will be necessary to fulfilling [TheDestiny](/TheDestiny), which is represented by extending the S into a "sprouting seed" with the original spaceship shape from [Brandon](/Brandon)'s emblem at the tip, a bit like a flower bud.  (Note: I chose not to include the extra flower petals suggested by Julie because they would detract from the spaceship serving as a lowercase L.  I did include petals in [the simpler version of the image](http://solseed.org/Image:Ben_SolSeed_Emblem_Simplified.jpg).)

*This is way cool Ben!* --[Brandon](/Brandon)
