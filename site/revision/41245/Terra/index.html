[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeedMovement]] < [[TruthMovement]] [[Image:WaterLandscape.jpg|150px]] [[Image:YellowLandscape.jpg|150px]] [[Terra]] is the third closest planet to [[Sol]], the sun in the center of our solar system. [[Terra]] is the earth we call home. Terra is also the [[personification]] of the non-living aspects of the Earth. Her relationship with [[Gaia]], who is the personification of the biosphere, the living aspect of the Earth, is intense and intimate. She is used by the SolSeed movement as a character in stories about Gaia.
