On Saturday, June 18, 2011, we went on a hike to beautiful Wahclella Falls in the Columbia gorge to celebrate the Summer Solstice (three days early). While at the falls, we discussed high points in our lives. The weather was looking questionable for the hike, but the rainclouds politely held off and only sprinkled us a little until after we got back in our cars to head home, at which point the downpour began. Then we had a lovely potluck dinner back at the [[Seed Village]].
