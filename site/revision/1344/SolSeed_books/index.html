''[[Happy in the Sun]]'' is a children's book that tells the story of a "curious little rocket" learning about the relationship between [[being]] and [[action|doing]]. We plan to self-publish it in early 2010. ''[[SolSeed book|Becoming SolSeed]]'' (working title) will be a longer, adult-oriented book describing our [[SolSeedPhilosophy|philosophy]], the [[SolSeedMovement]]'s plans, and our broader ideas about what a [[starfarers|starfaring]] civilization would look like and how we might build one. Our current [[OnePagePlan]] goal is to publish it by the end of 2010.
