== How close with Earthseed? ==
* John and Tony seem willing to combine efforts, with a few conditions attached
** Build it under the brand Earthseed (it seems to successfully attract people)
** John’s Conditions
*** Unapologetically a religion
*** Embrace theistic language (e.g., “God is Change”)
**** Possibly the biggest stumbling block for Ben, and to a lesser extent Brandon
**** Brandon thinks he can embrace it because the Gaia statue has shown the power of strong archetypes and personifications
** Tony's Conditions: unknown (he says "let's not reinvent the wheel")
* To Check
** Making images of God is Change (flying in the face of Shaper 1)
** In general, how do we update the text we're getting from these novels?
* Our Conditions
** No belief in anything supernatural
*** Eric is okay with involvement in more supernatural-friendly groups like Gaia's People Ottawa, but wouldn't abandon SolSeed if Earthseed has supernatural stuff in it
** Keeping The Destiny (aka Heaven) but perhaps moving toward calling it the Great Birthing
** Sustainability
** Life is Precious (emphasis)
* Brandon is willing to start over almost from the ground up in terms of the philosophy
* Eric plans to practice SolSeed until Earthseed catches up in terms of richness of practice
** John is okay with us throwing a lot of our ritual ideas, etc onto the Earthseed blog
* Ben: We should try to get John and Tony on one of these calls to talk about all this
* Brandon: If we move more toward Earthseed, we could maybe get some people like Kevin James Daniels back involved
