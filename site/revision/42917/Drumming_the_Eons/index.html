This is a ritual we celebrate every year on the Summer Solstice, starting at Sol 2012, to help experience the history of life on Earth by compressing the planet's lifespan to date into 18 minutes and 45 seconds. We use drums to represent the passage of millions of years, one million per drumbeat. One fact made clear by this experience is the dramatic acceleration of events as we get closer to the present, although this may be partly an artifact of the difficulty of studying the extremely deep past.
