- PlantCam at Seed 2011

  - Designed for natural light, so things look brown in tungsten lighting
- PhotoShop batch action to resize from 7 megapixels down to NTSC, 720x480
- Import output folder using Quicktime Pro Import Image Sequence feature at 15 fps
  - 1 minute 20 seconds
- Save as full frames or DV NTSC compression
