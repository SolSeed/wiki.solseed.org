== Summary of the April 21, 2009 [[SolSeedCall]] ==

[[Mark]], [[Ben]], [[Bonnie]], [[Brenna]], [[Bob]], [[Shelley]], and [[Brandon]] were all on the April 21 call.  We spent half the time discussing the first several chapters of [[The Untethered Soul]].  We're reading [[The Untethered Soul]] to prepare ourselves for [[Sol2009]].  I highly recommend reading it even if you don't join the calls or attend [[Sol2009]].  We finished the call by going over plans for [[Sol2009]] and creating a [[SteeringCommittee]] to lead the formation of a non-profit organization for the [[SolSeedMovement]].  The non-profit itself will have its first official meeting at [[Sol2009]].

We'd love to see you on the next call.  It's fun and never goes for more than 1 hour.

==Opening Words - (5 mins)
Mark: The SolSeedMovement exists to bring together and support all those who believe that Life is Precious. 

 Boni: (The greatest use of life is to spend it for something that outlasts it)


Brenna: It is about supporting each other in living the highest quality lives we can create,

 Boni:(Greatness lies not in trying to be somebody but in trying to help somebody.)

Bob: and more broadly it is about supporting and nurturing all of Life (including tomatoes, celery, and leeks), helping it thrive and grow. 

 Boni: (If the world seems cold to you, kindle fires to warm it)


Shelley: The members of the SolSeedMovement are striving to form a community, and working to develop a philosophy written in the form of a scripture and based on the central tenet that Life is Precious.


 Boni: (People who say that something is impossible should not interrupt those who are getting it done)


Ben: We believe that living according to this philosophy means becoming a specific kind of people we refer to as starfarers


Boni: (You cannot kindle a fire in another’s heart until it is burning in your own.)

=== [[Sol 2009]]: Who are we? ===

Ray of summer sun,
Inseparable from Sol,
Know yourself and thrive.

: '''Who are we as Life?'''
: '''Who are we as individuals on a spiritual journey?'''
: '''Who are we as members and friends of the SolSeed Movement?'''

Join us the weekend of June 27th 2009 at Cape Lookout State Park on the Pacific coast of Oregon as these "questions of being" shape our community and practice. 

At this family friendly weekend there will be camping, hotdogs, marshmallows, philosophizing, songs around the campfire, sand-castles, and the development of a core action plan for how we will "Bring Life" together.

=== Where ===
Cape Lookout State Park near Tillamook Oregon
* http://www.oregonstateparks.org/park_186.php
* So far we have reserved campsites C5, C6, C7, C9 and the meeting hall 

=== When ===
9AM Saturday June 27 to 1PM Monday June 29

You're welcome to arrive as early as Friday night (after 4 pm) and leave as late as Tuesday morning (before 1 pm).  Meals will be provided Friday supper through Monday breakfast.
