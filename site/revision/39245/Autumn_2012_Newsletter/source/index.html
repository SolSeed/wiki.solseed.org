'''This season, we focused in on our priority projects and took a few big steps.'''

<table>
<tr>
<td width="400px" valign="top">
[[Image:Rocket launch.JPG|400px]]

We all had a great time launching rockets to represent [[The Destiny]], as part of the Portland and [http://www.meetup.com/SolSeed-Ottawa/events/91063842/ Ottawa] Solstice celebrations on December 21st and 22nd.  The map of the United States on the asphalt was a nice touch, allowing the Portland contingent to imagine the city as the site of a major spaceport.  Each celebration also featured a potluck dinner with family and friends.
</td>
<td width="200px" valign="top">
[[Image:BookInHand-Open.jpg|200px]]

We moved toward publishing the big [[SolSeed book]] (title under review) by translating the text into LaTeX format, which allows us to define margins, title pages, and so on.  We even had a partial test copy printed to find out what it would look like.
</td>
<td width="200px" valign="top">
[[Image:Terra Lumina album cover.jpg|200px]]

The CD of songs about science that we helped to produce, [http://terralumina.bandcamp.com/album/terra-lumina Terra Lumina], was released on December 18th! John and Will should now have some time to work on the SolSeed song, using [[SolSeedSong|lyrics]] that I completed in November.
</td>
</tr>
</table>

This winter, we will: 
*continue work on the book 
*have a retreat to revisit and refine our overall purpose 
*go skating in February to celebrate Snowball Earth 
*hopefully get the finished song from John and Will and start working on a new music video! 


Thanks for reading!<br>
