The Exaltation is the last section of the [SolSeed Creed](/SolSeed_Creed).  It may be used instead of the creed when time or attention spans are short.

#### Exaltation
[Through](/Sign_for_Through) [Passion](/Sign_for_Passion), [Empathy](/Sign_for_Empathy), and [Wisdom](/Sign_for_Wisdom)
we have come to know that:

We are SolSeed
children of the [Earth](/Sign_for_Earth) and [Sun](/Sign_for_Sun)
awakened by starlight
growing
nurturing
discovering
We Bring Life!
