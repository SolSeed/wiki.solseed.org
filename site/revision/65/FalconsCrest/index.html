[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeedMovement]] < [[Seed2009]] < [[FalconsCrest]] [[Seed2009]] will be held at Falcon's Crest on Mount Hood 1 hour away from Portland Oregon. The lodge sleeps 24 and has lots of cozy spaces for [[OpenSpace]] sessions.
