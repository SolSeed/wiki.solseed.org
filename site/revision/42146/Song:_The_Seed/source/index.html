The draft lyrics for this [SolSeedSong](/SolSeedSong) were written by [Ben](/Ben). John Boswell and Will Crowley revised them, set them to music, and performed and recorded the song, as part of the reward they're giving to the [SolSeed Movement](/SolSeed_Movement) for our contribution to the [Terra Lumina kickstarter](http://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1769715853/an-album-all-about-science-terra-lumina).

### Structure
- 4 verses, each followed by a variant of the chorus
  - Verse 1: the self, [Passion](/Passion), spirituality, escape from routine
  - Verse 2: finding each other, [Empathy](/Empathy), building a [movement](/SolSeedMovement) and a [community](/SolSeedCommunity)
  - Verse 3: working with [kindred](/kindred) groups to protect [life](/life) on [Earth](/Earth) and help it thrive, [Wisdom](/Wisdom)
  - Verse 4: [TheDestiny](/TheDestiny), becoming the [seeds](/seeds) of [Gaia](/Gaia), growing new living worlds

### Final lyrics
#### Verse 1
It's hard to reach out for the stars  

From the inside of my cell.  

Trapped in my head, drifted so far  

From the story my heart wants to tell.  

It's a story of kinship, a story of roots  

And a glorious family tree.  

It's a story of passion, wings in the sky,  

possibilities yearning to be!

#### Chorus
I'm a seed - hidden deep and reaching for the sun.
I'm a seed - invisibly my journey has begun.
I'm a seed - I will find a way across the night.
I'm a seed - another world is growing toward the light.

#### Verse 2
I must join my tribe, and join hands around the fire.  

I must find the ones who naturally call me higher.  

We must dance as one, and open our eyes and move together.  

We are kindred and will be forever.  

A dance of compassion, a dance to inspire,  

To climb up toward the world we desire!

#### Verse 3
But each footprint we leave on the Earth  

Is a wound we don't know how to heal.  

We try to step lightly, but cannot be sure  

That our world will survive this ordeal.  

So it's time to rejoin the great pattern  

And learn wisdom as old as the sun.  

Then our cities and farms will be friends to the forests and seas  

And we'll flourish together as one!

#### Chorus

I'm a seed - invisibly my journey has begun.
I'm a seed - I will find a way across the night.
I'm a seed - another world is growing toward the light.
I must join my tribe.

#### Verse 4

Seeds blossoming in stone.  

Earth will send her children  

To grow themselves new homes.  

And when we meet the others,  

Seeds drifting from afar,  

We'll join them in their mission  

To take root amongst the stars!
