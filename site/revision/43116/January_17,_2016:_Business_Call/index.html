John Halstead's Earthseed tradition

  • Ben: Can we join without actually believing that God is Change? (I guess I'll have to ask John)
  • Brandon: Leave comments on the post too!
  • Brandon: What should our relationship with this be?
  • Ben: I think it depends on what the tradition turns out to be … so far it's an obscure website (http://godischange.org) and a few posts on Humanistic Paganism
  • Brandon: Of all the things out there to be a part of, this one is the closest
  • Ben: Other than maybe the Living Universe Foundation if it started up again?
  • Brandon: With two things competing for mindshare, it can be easy to get upset and focus on the differences … I want to nurture a feeling of being excited about the possibility that John's Earthseed tradition will take off … and I want you to support me and hold me to this declaration “I want to support this fledgling Earthseed with all I’ve got”
  • Eric: I agree that we should support it … what matters is not the name or the brand, it's the movement … we shouldn't shut down SolSeed today, but with the popularity of his site, there's a potential for John to really go somewhere with this, so we should be on board with that, and I think yeah, we should just join it, for the same reason that I didn’t hesitate to roll biospheric communionism into SolSeed.
  • Ben: I’d say there is no downside to declaring ourselves members whether or not it goes somewhere … and I hope that doing so means we get to help shape it
