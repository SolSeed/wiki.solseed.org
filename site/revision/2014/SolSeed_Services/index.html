__NOTOC__ The [[SolSeed Services]] are regular gatherings designed to be spiritual but not [[SolSeed and Religion|religious]], creating a space for the appreciation of [[Life]], of each other, and of the sacred. The goal is to create and nurture a sort of "spiritual [[SolSeedCommunity|community]]." The first four services, beginning in February 2010, were sizeable in-person gatherings in Portland, Oregon. Each of these had a "seed" topic that guides discussion as well as choices of music, poetry, and other [[Creativity|creative]] aspects. Then we decided to reduce the frequency of such gatherings to four times a year, at the Solstices and Equinoxes, and create weekly videoconference-based services for the core [[membership]].
