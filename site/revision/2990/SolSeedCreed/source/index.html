[[SolSeed]] < [[SolSeedMovement]] < [[SolSeedCreed]]

SolSeed offers its creed as a way to emotionally inspire people who embrace [[science]] and [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PT90dAA49Q reason], rather than superstition, as ways to know the world.

: ''There is [[SolSeedCreed/CallAndResponse|another version]] of the creed formatted for group reading.''
[[Image:SolSeedEmblemThumbnail.jpg | right]]

The [[SolSeedCreed]] is a statement of the shared values that ground the [[SolSeedMovement]].  The [[SolSeedCreed]] asserts that life and that which sustains life is good and precious, and calls humanity to mature into its role as caretaker of [[SolSeed]].  [[TheDestiny]] is the ultimate expression of what nurturing and protecting life means. Being worthy of and fulfilling [[TheDestiny]] are the two ultimate goals held by members of the [[SolSeedMovement]].

The [[SolSeedCreed]] also identifies empirically observable attributes of humanity that should be encouraged and balanced.  The [[SolSeedCreed]] calls us to nurture and balance our innate capacities for [[SelfLove]], [[Empathy]], and [[Wisdom]].

The [[SolSeedCreed]]
----

:Life is precious.  
::It has always been precious,
::it will always be precious.

:Life creates the conditions for more Life,
::in an Upward Spiral of ever-growing possibilities.

:As you are alive and I am alive,
:and in kinship with all other beings 
:nourished by Sol,
::we are SolSeed ---
::the Body of All Life.

:We believe 
:that The Destiny of SolSeed
:is to take root amongst the stars --- 
::to give birth to a family of living worlds.

:As SolSeed's intelligent spark,
::We are called to nurture Life,
::We are called to fulfill The Destiny.

:Our sacred duty is to
:embrace Passion,
:cultivate Empathy, and
:pursue Wisdom,
::So that our being honors SolSeed
::and our striving advances The Destiny.

:Passion drives us.
:Without Passion
::Empathy and Wisdom are impotent.
::
::I pledge to identify and channel my passion,
::to embrace my animal nature ---
::to be my true self, Happy in the Sun!

:Empathy is transcendent.
:Without Empathy,

::Passion and Wisdom are evil.
::I pledge to love others as I love myself,
::to consider their needs as if they were my own ---
::to Grow Ours, not just Get Mine!

:Wisdom is effective.
:Without Wisdom,

::Passion and Empathy are feeble and capricious.
::I pledge to seek truth by studying the world around me,
::to develop intuitive wisdom through regular practice ---
::to Cultivate Sound Instincts!

:Through Passion, Empathy, and Wisdom
:we have come to know that:

::We are SolSeed;
::Children of the sun,
::Awakened by starlight,
::Growing, nurturing, discovering,
::We Bring Life!
