### Books
- Brandon will create a publication plan this week to publish the children's book by Thanksgiving
- Brandon's vision for the bigger book:
  - Title: *SolSeed: The Book of Life*
  - Includes kid's book, big book, some hymns and poems
    - Useful as material for services
  - Publish a new version maybe every year ... only a few copies for core members
  - How to write it?
    - On the wiki? ... formatting and poem copyrights could be issues
    - Brandon hates working with MS Word
    - OpenOffice?
  - Parts of it (like the ["big book"](/SolSeed_book)) can be published in other ways
- Purpose of the "big book"
  - So people who resonate with [The Destiny](/The_Destiny) might discover us
    - Ex. we could a copy in each bag handed out to attendees of a convention
    - Carry copies around with us to hand out to people who might be kindred
    - Making it short and having a good hook in the intro and inspiring pictures would be good for this
  - To inspire the current members
    - Or at any rate, only people who are already really interested
    - In this version, the trifolds (and wiki) serve the purpose of initially drawing people in
    - Shelley is okay if we don't go this way as long as the bigger book, *The Book of Life,* is just for us
