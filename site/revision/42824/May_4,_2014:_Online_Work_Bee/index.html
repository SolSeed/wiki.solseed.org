Fourth Season

The fourth Liturgical Season runs from mid-spring to the summer solstice and covers the diversification of Life on land. The four advances are legs, amniotic eggs, wings, and flowering things.

Here is Eric and Ben's second draft of the opening ceremony for the Weekly Service Calls during this season:

A quiet forest... still ... a quiet forest... And then ... <gasp> ... Noisy animals!

Chirping Crickets, Roaring Dinosaurs, Calling Birds, Buzzing Bees.

Forests grow. In the lush green forests, diverse species are born. And for every species ... there is a story!

First came legs. Limbs to cross dry land, climb trees and explore the ever spreading forests Powerful legs to support the weight of the mighty dinosaurs!

Insects, and then reptiles, began laying eggs on land Air Breathing eggs Allowing Birth far from water Opening the heartland of continents to exploring animals!

Then came wings! Lifting insects, pterosaurs, birds and bats into the air, To escape predators, and strike prey from above To soar high, one step from space!

Next came the flowers, sharers of food. Both the nectar of bees, And the fruit from which early primates got their energy.

Legs, Land Eggs, Wings and Flowering Things

From few forms... Many forms!
