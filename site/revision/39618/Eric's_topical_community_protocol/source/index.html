[[Category:Topical Community Protocol]]

=== [[EricSaumur]]'s [[Topical Community Protocol | Protocol]] ===

====Meetup.com/solseed-ottawa====
* I will maintain one series of events on this site
** The season marking series: 8 Events per year 2 Solstices, 2 Equinoxes and 4 mid season events

====Facebook====
* I will make several distinct efforts on Facebook
** Friendings : I will make an average of one friend request per week until I reach 150 friends - Complete.
** SolSeed posts : I will share one SolSeed post per week
** Friend content likes/comments/shares : I will like, comment on or share at least one thing that a friend has put up per week.  There is a tendency to tit-for-tat in terms of likes: Like your friends stuff and they will like yours.
** Invite friends : I will invite two friends to like SolSeed each week.

==== Miscelaneous social activies ====
* Attending social activities creates oportunities to meet new people.  Eventually, I can explain SolSeed to them.  Also I can friend them on Facebook (see above).
** Other Meetups and Unitarian Universalist Services (I currently include the following meetups in this category: CFI, Less Wrong, Skeptics or Ottawa Activities, plus I also include Unitarian Universalist Services, ON/NO events and Overbrook Community Association meetings and events in this category.
** I will attend two events from this category every month.
** Other Social Activities : I will accept opportunities to attend social activities as they become available (accounting for conflicts of course)
* Other opportunities : I will break my protocol as necessary in order to take other actions that I perceive as more productive.  SolSeed is an experiment; it is new; it is better to try new methods as often as possible than to stick to the tried and true because we are too young to have a tried and true method.

=== Protocol Changes ===
I will change my protocol without notice if any of the following occur:
* I see a new oportunity that looks better than what I am doing.
* I find the pace is making me sick, exhausted or otherwise is not bringing life to me
* I find the pace is interfering with my family or work life and is not bringing life to them.
