An alternate [[SolSeed Emblem]] concept proposed by [[User:BenSibelman|Ben]]. Ideas: Starting from the image of sunrise as viewed from space, I created a symbolic version of [[Terra]] with an S marking out a yin-yang of technology (city lights) and [[Life|nature]] (clouds), trying to show them as intertwined and equal. Both will be necessary to fulfilling [[TheDestiny]], which is represented by extending the S into a "sprouting seed" with the original spaceship shape from [[Brandon]]'s emblem at the tip, a bit like a flower bud. (Note: I chose not to include the extra flower petals suggested by Julie because they would detract from the spaceship serving as a lowercase L. I did include petals in [http://solseed.org/Image:Ben_SolSeed_Emblem_Simplified.jpg the simpler version of the image].) :: ''This is way cool Ben!'' --[[Brandon]] ::: Very cool! And, I also really like the simplified version. Even the least artistic among us could draw that one ourselves. [[User:TedErnst|TedErnst]]
