The [[SolSeedMovement]] has attracted people who have little use for a literal interpretation of [[TheDestiny]] or even for space travel as an inspiring story, but who feel drawn to participate by the group's strong [[values]] and friendly atmosphere.  Others may find their interests align quite well, but simply need some time to learn more about [[SolSeed]] and decide whether [[JoiningTheMovement|becoming a member]] is right for them.  All these people can be defined as [[FriendsOfSolSeed]], and belong to the much larger group of [[Kindred]] who share the values of the [[SolSeedMovement]].

If you are a [[FriendOfSolSeed]] who wants to be kept up to date on our doings every month, please add a link to your userpage below.
