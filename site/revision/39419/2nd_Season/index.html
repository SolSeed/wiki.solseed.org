The second liturgical season of the SolSeed movement runs from mid-winter (approximately February 5th) to the spring equinox. It tells the story of the evolution of life on Earth from the development of multicellularity to the Cambrian Explosion. In the first liturgical season there were four major advances: the ignition of Sol, the agglomeration of the Earth, the Condensation of Water and the invention of Photosynthesis. What would the four major advances of the second season be? Some ideas might be: the invention of organs, the invention of calcification (teeth, armour, bones, coral), the invention of the mouth, the invention of the anus?, the invention of symmetry, the melting of snowball earth.
