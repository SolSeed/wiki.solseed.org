'''The Tulipathon''' is the [[Multifaith Housing Initiative]](MHI)'s main annual fundraising event.  Faith groups gather their members to walk with signs declaring their support for MHI and for affordable housing.  It is a fundraiser rather than a protest, with members gathering sponsorships to walk the 6 km course.  [[Eric]] walked carrying a sign declaring SolSeed's support for MHI, [[Patrick]] carried a sign declaring the [[Unitarian Universalist Fellowship of Ottawa]]'s support for MHI and [[Michelle]] walked carrying a sign declaring [[Saint Paul's Eastern United Church]]'s support for MHI.
