The SolSeed Book Program has two parts.  First, we hand out free copies of the [[Parable of the Sower]] that each have a sticker with a unique web address for the book so that we can track its journey.  People who receive free copies of the book are asked to pass them along to someone else after they have read the book.  The second part of the SolSeed Book Program is the organization of [[Book Group]]s that read and discuss books that inspire and strengthen [[Values]] that are consistent with the [[SolSeedMovement]].
