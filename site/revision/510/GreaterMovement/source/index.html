There is a movement.  This is true whether or not we recognize and name it.  We choose to name it the [[GreaterMovement]] so that we can consciously support it.

We are each composed of and shape the flows around us.  This is life.  We live.  Our universe lives.

Sometimes flows [[BringLife]] and sometimes they [[DrainLife]].  We call the parts of ourselves and the universe that routinely [[BringLife]] "the [[GreaterMovement]]".  Every individual and organization is sometimes growing the [[GreaterMovement]] and sometimes sapping it.

We name as [[Kindred]] those individuals and organizations whose wise actions embody the values and principles of the [[GreaterMovement]] and inspire us to deepen our practice.
