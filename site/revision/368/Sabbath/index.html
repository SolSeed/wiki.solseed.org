Keeping Sabbath is one potential SolSeed [[How can I bring life in my consumption - or lack thereof? | praxis]] -- a way to live so that our beliefs are not just theory but action. Sabbath is a rest, or a sacred time set aside for deepening. On Sabbath we spontaneously follow the spirit's call, intentionally nurture our inner lives, and strengthen our ties to family and friends.
