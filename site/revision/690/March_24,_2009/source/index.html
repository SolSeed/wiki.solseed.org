== Summary of the March 17, 2009 [[SolSeedCall]] ==

[[Mark]], [[Stacey]], [[Brenna]], [[Ben]], [[Shelley]], and [[Brandon]] were all on the March 24 call.  After [[OpeningWords]] and checking in with each other about how we are doing, we spent the majority of the call discussing [[WhatConnectsUsAll]].  When a lady asked her about what her SolSeed jacket represents, [[Brenna]] felt "it would have been nice if I had something concise and inspiring to tell her."  [[Ben]] feels that [[TheDestiny]] is what makes us special and that he finds that inspiring.  [[Shelley]] "finds it difficult to share [[TheDestiny]] with people in my life".  [[Brenna]] is a member of the [[SolSeedMovement]] for the community, not for [[TheDestiny]].  We were just getting rolling on the topic when we reached the end of the call so we decided to carry it over to the next call.

We'd love to see you on the next call.  It's fun and never goes for more than 1 hour.

== Opening words - 5 mins ==

MARK:
The SolSeed Creed is a statement of the shared values that ground the SolSeed Movement.  The SolSeed Creed asserts that life and that which sustains life is good and precious, and calls humanity to mature into its role as caretaker of SolSeed.  The Destiny is the ultimate expression of what nurturing and protecting life means.  Being worthy of and fulfilling The Destiny are the two ultimate goals held by members of the SolSeed Movement.
