Sophia is the Goddess of Wisdom, that is she is the personification of wisdom.  The current best seat of wisdom is the human mind so she is also the personification of the human mind.  Given that the human mind is divided in many ways {conscious/subconscious, left/right, Monkey/Mammal/Lizard/Fish/Old Jelly Fish, speaker/listener} Sophia manifests in stories as multiple little girls sometimes riding an elephant.  She tends to represent rational wisdom rather than instinctive wisdom and so she tends to represent the left/right, speaker/listener, competing opinions multiplicities of the mind most often.  She may also represent the multiplicity of rational opinion across multiple humans.  The various bodies of Sophia spend a lot of time arguing with each other but tend to speak as one voice when talking to [[You]].
