#### A lot about me
Some folks have asked me why I add my two middle initials when I sign my name. I do it to make it easy to tell me apart from the more famous folks that also have the name Brandon Sanders. My four names are a brief sketch of my family tree. The first three names come from the gals and the last name comes from the fellas:

 - || maternal Grandma born Evelina Brandon.

 - || paternal Grandma born Delores Clark.

 - || wife born Shelley Schoepflin.

 - || dad born Robert Sanders.

I'm proud of my family and the sacrifices they've made so that I could have more opportunities than they.

My dad is a journeyman model maker who runs 4B, a small [Wire EDM](http://www.4bformtools.com) shop. I inherit my creativity and passion for inventing from him. My mom is a journeyman die maker who nurtures our family and does the books for 4B.  I inherit my attention to detail and passion for justice from her. Thanks mom and dad!
