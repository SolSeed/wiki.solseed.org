=== Current SolSeed Values listed on our [[OnePagePlan]] ===

* [[Life]] is precious ... being alive ... happy in the sun
* [[The Destiny]] of SolSeed is to take root amongst the stars (openness to audacious [[possibilities]]/optimism)
* Be [[starfarer]] now (openness to audacious possibilities/optimism)
* Presence ... mindfulness ... in the now
* Nurture body, mind and spirit ([[SelfLove]]) 
* We ALL are precious SolSeed (openness/welcoming differences) ([[SolSeedCommunity|community]]) ([[Empathy]]) 
* Acting Upstream ... picking small actions that have large downstream impact ... like Buckminster Fuller's trim-tab concept ([[Wisdom]])
