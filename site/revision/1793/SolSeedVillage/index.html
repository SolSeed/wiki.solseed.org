The [[SolSeedVillage]] is a planned eco-village to be built somewhere in East Portland, Oregon. In rough outline, it will be a cohousing community with some dorm-style living for volunteers, substantial food-producing gardens, and workshops including spaces dedicated to one or more space-related industries. Sustainability and green building techniques will be central to its design. == Visualizations == * The current plan is to start where we are, with [[Brandon]] and [[Shelley]]'s house and backyard, and expand from there. The first step will be to build a [http://www.seattle.gov/dpd/Planning/Backyardcottages/Overview/ backyard cottage] which might look something like this: [[Image:Backyard cottage rough visualization.jpg]] * We got a bunch of visitors at an [[Earth Day 2010]] event in Portland to participate in painting a mural with ideas for the eco-village. Here is the final result: [[Image:Earth Day 2010 eco-village mural.jpg]] * [[Ben]] created a few brief shots of one concept for what the [[SolSeedVillage]] might one day look like, for use in his video [[Pick a Future]] and possibly for banners to be displayed at events.
