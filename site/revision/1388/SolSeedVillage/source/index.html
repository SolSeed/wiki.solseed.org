The [[SolSeedVillage]] is a planned eco-village to be built somewhere in East Portland, Oregon.  In rough outline, it will be a cohousing community with some dorm-style living for volunteers, substantial food-producing gardens, and workshops including spaces dedicated to one or more space-related industries.  Sustainability and green building techniques will be central to its design.
