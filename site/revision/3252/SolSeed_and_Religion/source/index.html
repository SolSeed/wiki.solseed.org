== Is SolSeed a religion? ==

[[SolSeed]] is currently reconsidering whether or not to [[Legal Organization|organize]] as a religion.  We're certainly not a traditional religion, given that we embrace [[science]] and don't believe in gods or supernatural forces, but we are also embracing the power of [[SolSeedServices|religious ritual]] as an effective psychological tool to motivate ourselves.  This ritual helps us be happier through feeling connected to others and to a cause that's larger than ourselves, and regularly motivates us to meet our commitments.  We also consider "reverence for [[life]]" as a core tenet, while looking forward to the almost spiritual [[Destiny]] of acting as seeds to spread life to other worlds.

Here is a [http://www.xtranormal.com/watch/11077454/ dialogue] that would help explain why we chose to become a religion, if that turns out to be our decision.  If so, we might use a metaphor to explain what kind of religion we are and avoid mistaken assumptions: "SolSeed is a branch of the [[Being a Starfarer#Truth, Beauty, and Values (the religion of science)|Church of Science]]."

== Can I join the [[SolSeedMovement]] and keep my existing religion? ==

Regardless of the above decision, SolSeed makes no statements about God or the supernatural, other than that we, the founding members ([[Brandon]], [[Shelley]], and [[Ben]]), ''don't'' believe in such things.  In looking for new [[members]], we don't discriminate on the basis of religion (though we do discourage [[magical thinking]]), and we think the ideals and goals of [[SolSeed]] ought to be integratable without conflict into any kind of faith.
