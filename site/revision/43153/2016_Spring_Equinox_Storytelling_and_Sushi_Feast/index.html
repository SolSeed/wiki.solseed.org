Our 2016 Spring Equinox celebration was held on Sunday March 20th in Redmond and Gatineau. We added board games as a new element, with the West Coast contingent playing Evolution and the Saumurs playing Terra Mystica: Fire & Ice.

Stories

  • Brandon presented his AI horror story, Abe's Inquisition
  • Shelley shared her lighthearted tale, Spider Momma
  • Patrick told an amusing tale about an inept wizard and a druid
  • Eric told a science fiction story about Dyson Trees, Seed Fall
  • Ben presented his talk for the Contact Conference about Biospheric Communionism
