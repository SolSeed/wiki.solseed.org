Our 2016 Spring Equinox celebration was held on Sunday March 20th in Redmond and Gatineau. We added board games as a new element, with the West Coast contingent playing [Evolution](http://www.northstargames.com/products/evolution) and the Saumurs playing [Terra Mystica: Fire & Ice](https://diceygoblin.com/blog/terra-mystica-fire-ice-review/).

### Stories
- [Brandon](/Brandon) presented his AI horror story, [Abe's Inquisition](/Abe's_Inquisition)
- [Shelley](/Shelley) shared her lighthearted tale, [Spider Momma](/Spider_Momma)
- Patrick told an amusing tale about an inept wizard and a druid
- [Eric](/Eric) told a science fiction story about Dyson Trees, [Seed Fall](/Seed_Fall)
- [Ben](/Ben) presented his [talk for the Contact Conference](https://onedrive.live.com/view.aspx?resid=5FC9CD5EA201996E!3577&ithint=file%2cdocx&app=Word&authkey=!ANyLuSxWHdkuHMk) about [Biospheric Communionism](https://sites.google.com/site/biosphericcommu/)
