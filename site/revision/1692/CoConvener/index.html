For [[OpenSpace]] events, [[CoConvener]]s agree to do the following: : 1) attend and bring energy to the event : 2) send invitations to folks they think will also bring good energy and whom they would like to hang out with : 3) chip in on the logistics In addition to these essential agreements, [[CoConvener]]s often help shape the initial invitation.
