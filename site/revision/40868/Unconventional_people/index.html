''This was a session at the [[Shiny Green Acorn Festival]].'' == Participants == * [[Brandon]] (convener) * [[Ben]] == Discussion == Brandon divides [[unconventional people]] into two categories: * Preconventional: People who never made it in the mainstream world ** They offer time and energy, but not conventional wisdom or financial resources *** Ben: But they might have other types of wisdom * Postconventional: People who have been successful in the mainstream and know how to go back to it if needed ** They offer wisdom about working with the mainstream, resources, ''and'' time and energy if they no longer have mainstream jobs * Brandon: There are many more preconventionals than postconventionals ** Ben: There may be more disaffected middle-class people, looking for ways to live differently from their mainstream lives, than you think
