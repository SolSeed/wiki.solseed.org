[[Sol 2013]] was planned as a three-day retreat to discuss the overall direction of the [[SolSeed Movement]] and practical next steps for moving in that direction.

== Schedule of sessions and events ==
=== Wednesday June 19 ===
* [[Ben]] travels to Ottawa and is met by [[Eric]], who takes him home by bus
* The Sanders travel to Seattle to obtain passports for [[Sequoia]] and [[Ren]]
* Session in the car: [[The Gospel According to Shelley]]

=== Thursday June 20 ===
* The Sanders travel to Ottawa
* Ben and Eric visit the [http://nature.ca/ Museum of Nature] and play board games

=== Friday June 21 ===
* Shortly after midnight: Ben and Eric meet the Sanders at the airport and drive to the Saumur family's cabin at Norway Lake
* Session: [[Who do we want to become?]]
* Eric goes back to Ottawa to pick up [[Michelle]] and [[Patrick]]
* [[Summer Solstice ritual]]
* Session: [[Who do we want to become continued]]
* Session: [[Global challenges]]

=== Saturday June 22 ===
* [[SolSeed Service]] with a reading from the [[SolSeedCreed]] v3.0.0 commentary
* [[YayLifeTri]]
* Session: [[Arguments about morality]]
* Session: [[Planning experiments to build membership]]
* Session: [[Adjusting the SolSeedCreed v3.0.0]]
* Everyone but Michelle returns to Eric's home in Ottawa
* Session in the van: [[How utopian are we?]]

=== Sunday June 23 ===
* Eric drops off the Sanders at the airport and picks up Michelle at the cabin
* Ben and Patrick play board games, later joined by Eric and Michelle
* Eric and Patrick accompany Ben to the airport by bus
