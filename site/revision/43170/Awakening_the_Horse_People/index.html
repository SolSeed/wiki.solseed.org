What Awakening the Horse People does

  • Work with European-descended people to help them learn their original cultures
  • Where knowledge has been “lost,” go to ancestral places and listen to the Earth and animals, which store the knowledge
  • But you have to learn from indigenes how to do it, because Europeans have been separated from place and traumatized for thousands of years
  • Work with ecovillages to start a movement to return land to indigenous peoples
  • Looking for temporary membership at Tryon to work on this

    • Response to SolSeed
