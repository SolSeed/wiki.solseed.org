Geography
* East of Portland: Windward, Camp Trackers, Camp Namanu
* South of Portland: Silver Falls, Camp Westwind, Cape Lookout
* Also consider Cedar Ridge, which is an hour northwest of Portland
* Also consider the Whidbey Institute, which is northwest of Seattle on Whidbey Island
