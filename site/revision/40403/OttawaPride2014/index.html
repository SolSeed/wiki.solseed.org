Two members of [[SolSeed]], [[Patch | Patrick]] and myself, [[Eric]], attended Ottawa's Pride Parade on August 24th, 2014. Patrick marched with the Unitarians while I pedaled the SolSeed PrideMobile in loops beside and around them. We marched with the Unitarians because we are both members of the [www.uufo.org Unitarian Universalist Fellowship of Ottawa]. The reason that I rode a bike was because I sprained my ankle yesterday working in the garden. Walking would have been painful. But bicycling added a lot of movement and interest to the otherwise poorly attended Unitarian banner. [[File:SolSeedPrideMobile.jpg|400px|right| [[Patch]] standing in front of the SolSeed PrideMobile locked to a post outside the celebration at the end of the parade]] The SolSeed PrideMobile was simple Eric's bike with three copies of a quickly created SolSeed Pride Poster attached to the milk crate on the back: [[File:PridePoster.jpg|400px|left|thumb|Close up of poster used to decorate the PrideMobile]] I am proud to have shown support for LGBTQ+ rights while carrying both the SolSeed and Unitarian names. Accepting, in fact embracing, diversity is an important part of both philosophies.
