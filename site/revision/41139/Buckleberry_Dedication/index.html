Between September 17th and 24th, 2017 the [[SolSeed Movement]] gathered in the Ottawa-Gatineau area. By the 21st we were all gathered at [[Buckleberry]] in time for the Equinox. We climbed the hill and performed a dedication ritual in which we painted organism tattoos onto her using spray paint and stencils. We took turns performing the ritual. Each person walked up to the statue with a stencil and paint colour of their choice and said a line of SolSeed Movement scripture. If the line was a call from the Creed, then the congregation responded. Then the person painted the image where they wanted. Over the course of the morning we added images of members from every Kingdom of Life.
