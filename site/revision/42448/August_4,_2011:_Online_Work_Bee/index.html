Description of 350

"350" is a reference to what scientists have determined is a safe level of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere (measured in parts per million), one that will likely prevent the global climate from spiraling out of control. Problem is, we're currently above 390 parts per million and still rising. We need to reverse that trend, and soon.
