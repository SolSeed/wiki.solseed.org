The eight liturgical seasons of the SolSeed Movement represent a special kind of [[SolSeed Cosmic Calendar | Cosmic Calendar]] * 1 - [[Genesis]] Winter Solstice to Mid Winter * 2 - [[Conflatorium]] Mid Winter to Spring Equinox * 3 - [[Viriditas de Terra]] Spring Equinox to Mid Spring * 4 - [[Diversitas]] Mid Spring to Summer Solstice * 5 - [[5th Season]] Summer Solstice to Mid Summer (draft) * 6 - [[6th Season]] Mid Summer to Fall Equinox (draft) * 7 - [[Viventibus Mundos]] Fall Equinox to Mid Fall * 8 - [[Viventibus Galaxia]] Mid Fall to Winter Solstice
