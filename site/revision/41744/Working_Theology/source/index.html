The SolSeed Movement is a truth, beauty and values movement. It is not a [religion](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religion), but it shares some of the functions of a religion by providing structure and language to support human spiritual growth. Members of the SolSeed movement embrace a diversity of faiths and belief systems that share a common theme: [Life is Precious](/Life_is_Precious).

Members of the SolSeed movement continue to embrace and deepen our commitment to various non-faith and faith traditions through our participation in SolSeed. We use the pages listed below to construct working theologies (or "a-theologies") that articulate our beliefs through the lens we all share: [Life is Precious](/Life_is_Precious).
