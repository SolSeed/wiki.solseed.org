== Daily practice ==
* Ben questions the video practice: “I’ve haven’t been diligent in sending. You haven’t sent any. And I’m not sure you’re even watching the ones that I’ve sent.”
** Eric: I watched the ones in previous weeks but this week has been crazy and I haven’t watched any.
** Brandon: Do you have a recommendation?  Is this a place we want to be going.  If it isn’t we can cut it.  If it is we need to motivate the elephant, ___ the rider, and/or tweak the path to make it work for our elephants

* Three daily practices:
** Meditation
*** Why?
**** Brandon: Connectedness to values and to each other.
*** Problems
**** Brandon's path is to do it on the bus, but he worked from home every day this week except for a day when Eric had a meeting … it's mostly working

** 15 minutes on projects
*** Why?
**** Brandon: strongly aspiring to be diligent in working on SolSeed every day
*** Solutions
**** Eric: Each have a different path based on when we do it
**** Brandon: If I go to bed on time and get up before the kids do, I have time to do the work in the morning

** 1 minute videos
*** Why?
**** Eric: Pat ourselves on the back for doing the work, get recognition from each other
**** Ben: Stay in touch with each other more regularly despite the fact that we're working on these different individual projects
*** Problems
**** Brandon isn't doing this because he isn't doing the projects
**** Eric’s path for the videos comes at the end of the 15 minutes of work, so if he misses the 15 minutes of work he misses the video … or if he overdoes the work (maybe 90 minutes) then he has to dart away to the next thing before he can do the video
**** Ben is like Brandon in that if he doesn’t do any work on his story he doesn't feel like posting a video (and late at night as well).
*** Solutions
**** Eric: Create space around/during the meditation calls for this
**** Eric: Remind ourselves that we should feel welcomed to do the video even if we haven't done the 15 minutes on projects that day
**** Brandon: We could watch and record the videos at the start of our 15 minutes, to organize our thoughts about what we're going to work on … it's a momentum-builder
