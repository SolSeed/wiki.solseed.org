The [[Shiny Green Acorn Festival]] unconference was held from August 12-14, 2016, largely at the Sanders home in Aranmore and at [http://tryonfarm.org Tryon Life Community Farm]. The goal of the event was to celebrate as a community and plan playfully. == Focus question == It’s been 18 years since [[Octavia Butler]] published ''Parable of the Talents''. What can we learn from Acorn?
