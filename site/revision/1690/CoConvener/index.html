For [[OpenSpace]] events, [[CoConvener]]s agree to do the following: : 1) attend and bring energy to [[Kindred2010]] : 2) send invitations to folks you'd like to hang out with : 3) chip in on the logistics In addition to these essential agreements, [[CoConvener]]s often help shape the initial invitation.
