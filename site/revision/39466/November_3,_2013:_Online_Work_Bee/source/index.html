=== Children International ===
* A charity that gives you some contact with the people you're helping ("your chance to build a lifelong friendship with an impoverished child")
** For $25 per month, you become a sponsor for a single child
* Eric has heard of a religious group that does something similar, but drops support for any child who becomes pregnant because she's a "sinner"
** Children International doesn't appear to be religious anymore, but Eric thinks the group he's thinking of hides their religious nature to some degree

* Proposal: We sponsor 4 children (one per active member) for $100US a month ... Shelley has ability to veto this once we pass it.
* Discussion:
* Ben: look at website, see what we think.  Secular organization (didn't used to be) ... they support "all faiths".
* Brandon and Eric: The organization Eric was concerned about is probably Compassion International
* Brandon: It would be pretty lousy to withdraw our support before they've grown up
* Vote: Unanimous support for the proposal as amended with veto for Shelley

=== Website Design Help ===

* Proposal: We've had 104 clicks at a cost of $121.27 so far.  Not one of those 104 has signed up on the email list.  Brandon is ready to put in some time on the website design (http://join.solseed.org ) and he would like to set aside $1500 of virtual for use to hire help on the design as needed.
What say ye?

* Discussion:
** Ben: do we want to experiment more with ad wording first?
** Brandon: We don't need more clicks, just more conversions of the clicks
** Brandon: I imagine doing a bunch of iterations of the website, slowly using up the allocated $1500 (an image here, a graphic design consultant there (odesk, vworker, etc))
** Ben: Counterproposal, before we approve this, Ben takes a stab at this ... basically let's do what we can for free (because it would be easy to improve the existing site)
** Brandon prefers moving toward a professional site rather than getting stuck at an amateur local maximum
** Eric: We have some money now, none of us have a huge amount of time.  The 4 of us are the only people who can generate ideas for SolSeed.  We can't hire people to "come up with SolSeed" for us.  But we can hire people to do graphics, etc to help us.  Let's hire people to do "X" for us so that we can focus on SolSeed.
** Ben: If we had six members and I had a choice between being on the governing committee and being a graphic designer, I would rather be the graphic designer.  Brandon, you speak about supporting passionate amateurs.
** Brandon: I would like you to work on the music video.
** Ben: but that's harder (plaintively).
** Eric: What if Brandon took over "The Seed" music video and Ben took over the website design?
** Ben: I do have some very definite ideas of what I want to put in the music video
** Eric: I can't imagine you'd have the time to do both projects
** Ben: I can't imagine a single iteration on this simple website taking that much of my time, but many iterations would get time consuming
** Eric: I think we want to do many iterations.
** Brandon: Ben, is the money the issue, or that you don't want someone else doing the website?
** Ben: I say that I'm SolSeed's media guy, and I feel bad about turning over the website to some outsider
** Eric: Are you worried about losing the title of media guy within SolSeed?
** Ben: If we're going to say that my work isn't professional enough for the outward facing website, that hurts my self esteem a little.  After all, we've been putting images that we created almost from scratch on our wiki for a long while, and now you are saying that we need to turn it over to someone who knows better than us what looks good.
** Brandon: I think we should take a week to think about this and come back to it.
