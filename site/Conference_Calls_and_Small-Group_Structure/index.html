This Sol 2009 session was convened for the purpose of figuring out where to go next with the SolSeedCalls and other smaller meetings between the large OpenSpace events.

Participants

  • Brandon CS Sanders
  • Bonnie Sanders
  • Brenna Waxter
  • Regula Frey
  • Ben Sibelman

Review of conference calls so far

  • Regula: Opening words and checkin time = leaving regular life behind and connecting on a heart level before moving into projects (ideas from the humanist movement meetings)
  • Bonnie: Provided continuity of group even though not everyone was present on every call
  • Helped get to know people at a deep level
  • Brenna, Regula: Women especially need to connect at a heart level
  • But this ideal shouldn't come at the expense of having another purpose

Ideas for the future

  • Brenna: We could recruit people to build small local groups that meet in person
  • When one gets too big, have one person split off and start a new group?
  • Regula: Similar to the humanist movement in Africa
  • But should use the term "invite" rather than "recruit"
  • Each person can try to form a local group and bring that experience back to the call
  • Specific ideas for call
  • Just one topic per call
  • Move more practical stuff to a once-a-month business call
  • Do longer checkins after unconference to discuss how we experienced it
