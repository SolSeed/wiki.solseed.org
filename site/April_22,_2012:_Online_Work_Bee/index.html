Celebrate Evolution Solstice Triathlon

  • Do it early
  • 9am ... avoid heat; runners like it cool
  • Short to encourage participation ...
  • A try-a-tri is 375 m swim, 10km bike, run 2.5 km
  • Normally for Michelle, Patrick and I we go even shorter: 750 m swim, 1 km run, 6 km bike
  • I suggest for little kids we go even shorter: 100 meter swim (with option to wade), 400 meter run, 1.5 km bike/skateboard/rollerblade
  • Official triathlon puts run after bike to reduce danger when people are really tired
  • We need to put swim first for symbolism se below. So even more important to keep distances short
  • Order of operations and symbolism
  • Before ... fresh fruits and vegetables (no technology)
  • Swim first ... life began in the oceans
  • Run Next ... life moved onto the land
  • Bike Next ... Then humanity added technology enabling SolSeed to venture to the stars
  • After ... highest technology cooking system available (chocolate fondue ;))
  • Philosophy
  • About participation rather than an olympic event
  • Call it the Celebrating Evolution Solstice Family Fun Triathlon
  • T-shirts or other freebees with the short story of evolution image; something like: fish, amphibian, monkey, bicyclist
