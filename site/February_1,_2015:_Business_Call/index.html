  • Good public transportation
  • Near existing schools (until we start our own)
  • Enough space for all contributing members to live there
  • Internet access
  • Inspiration … it's got to be exciting enough to overcome our inertia
  • Green building features
  • Beautiful sacred meeting space/Temple
  • Food & flower garden
  • Stirring natural setting and/or vibrant urban setting (e.g. Forest Park)
  • Non-mandatory but still important features
  • Common spaces for meals
  • Bikable
  • Workshop
  • Good multi-purpose meeting space
