The Shiny Green Acorn Festival unconference was held from August 12-14, 2016, largely at the Sanders home in Aranmore and at Tryon Life Community Farm. The goal of the event was to celebrate as a community and plan playfully.

Focus question

It’s been 18 years since Octavia Butler published Parable of the Talents. What can we learn from Acorn? What do we envision for our Acorn-like ecovillage in the Portland area or elsewhere?
