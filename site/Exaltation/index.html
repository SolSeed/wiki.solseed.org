The Exaltation is the last section of the SolSeed Creed. It may be used instead of the creed when time or attention spans are short.

Exaltation

Through Passion, Empathy, and Wisdom we have come to know that:

We are SolSeed children of the Earth and Sun awakened by starlight growing nurturing discovering We Bring Life!
