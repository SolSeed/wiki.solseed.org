Spiritual Experience Experiments

  • List of ideas for experiences: Direct experiences that lead to religiousness
  • What would the experiments look like?
  • Eric: Is the experience of watching a movie that supports the upward spiral distinguishable from that of watching one that supports the downward spiral ("filled with orange explosions that teach us to cheer for death")?
  • ''Avatar" does both
  • The value judgments involved lead to a temptation to twist the data toward what we want it to say
  • Ideally we'd have randomly chosen research subjects who don't know which group they're in
  • Ben: How do we handle the subjectivity of the data? Is there a baseline we can reliably measure against?
  • Eric: Break up the problem into smaller chunks, e.g. compare movies about revenge with movies about building a relationship
  • Ben: Each participant would probably have to watch both types of movie
  • Questionnaire design
  • Ben: Degree of epiphany or life-changingness seems difficult to ask about in a coherent, scientifically valid way
  • Eric: Ask about it 6 different ways and use a 5-point rating scale (Eric read about this technique in a literature review paper)
  • Eric: We should do a literature review ourselves (which might require money for accessing the relevant articles, although we could use Patrick's university account)
