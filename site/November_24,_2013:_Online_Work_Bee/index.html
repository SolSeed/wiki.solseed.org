SolSeed Soundbite

  • Ben: "SolSeed is a tiny quasi-religious movement. We believe that all life is precious, and that we should help it thrive, grow, and spread to the stars." Meets the criteria of 27 words, 9 seconds, 3 points.
  • The overloading of the word SolSeed causes a hitch in my giddyup when I'm listening to what you are saying.
  • Ben: the term "quasi-religious movement" is a bit awkward ... Brandon and Eric are using different terms ... I'm not sure exactly what I want to use
  • Keith: Maybe you need a new word altogether (they do this all the time in German)
  • Brandon: "SolSeed is a cosmic Wisdom Tradition whose central tenet is that Life, in all its messiness, is worthy of veneration. We seek to express Life's vitality through our practice, both individual and collective."

Later on his own, Brandon made the following revision:

  • "SolSeed is the body of all life. As a SolSeed Wisdom Tradition we have as our central tenet the judgement that Life, in all its messiness, is worthy of veneration. We seek to express Life's vitality through our practice, both individual and collective."
  • Ben: "We have our central tenet the judgement that" could probably be cut down to two words or so
  • Keith: judgement sounds like a negative word
  • Not including the space part: Brandon would like to have the conversation about how life's nature points toward expanding beyond Earth later, after establishing more common ground with the person he's talking to
  • Eric: "SolSeed is an agnostic religion. We believe that all Life is precious, that we should nurture lives, help Life thrive on Earth, and spread to the stars."
