Review Shiny Green Festival

These were great, let’s be sure to keep these elements if/when we do it again:

  • Hiking
  • Water (this time we did Poet’s Beach)
  • Halsteads
  • Drumming the Eons (at council crest or some other awesome location)
  • Hemisphere
  • Delivered Dish (for small catering needs)
  • Hold during a dance festival (for Michelle)

Things to do next time that we didn't do this time:

  • Providence Bridge Pedal
  • Visit OMSI
  • Get Steven Wolfe and others (may help to schedule at a less vacation-heavy time of year)
  • A session at Marquam Shelter
  • Sessions in pedal bikes that take up to 6 people on the waterfront
  • More participatory/hands-on sessions involving some long-term project, e.g. greenhouses, robots, Kickstarter design
  • Ritual to honor our losses (perhaps belongs at the winter solstice?)
  • Try to balance out sessions by people who tend to talk a lot with discussions where it's easier for everyone to contribute (may not be a big issue since conversations on the trail were more like the latter)

Venues we liked/didn’t like:

  • DeWeese classroom … expensive, but nice to have the indoor space … probably worth it for midsize (12-20) gatherings
  • 4T
  • Vietnam Memorial … pretty, has sun and shade, hills are a little steep
  • Council Crest … great views and some shade, but hard to do both at once ... interesting because highest point in Portland … crowded
  • Food Carts … good place to get lunch, but not a great place to talk … perhaps combine with Director Park next time or just get lunch at Director Park?
  • Poet's Beach … a bit noisy, but a good place to relax, cool off, and have fun
  • Grotto … beautiful, but a little shush-y … not completely comfortable taking over a location for an in-depth session … they do have a conference center, investigate whether we could use it

Other thoughts:

  • Brandon … didn’t really seem like a festival, more like our traditional Open Space … perhaps book a musical act or two and really make it a festival … because it was spread out it was more of a “team-building” exercise than a focused Open Space event … difficult to balance the wide range of expectations and needs
  • Ben … it felt like we didn't make much progress on our focus question ... or on the movie project I wanted to launch there … but it still felt worthwhile

Are we going to try again with the Shiny Green Festival?

  • Brandon … not ready to decide, still need to let my subconscious chew on it a bit more
  • Ben … likewise, but leaning toward yes … I love the idea of Shiny Green and want to work on gathering more people who are also into it
